## RESEARCH BASELINE (real demand signals found)

**1. The "no village" gap in the diaspora.** In Nigeria and across Africa, the *omugwo* tradition gives new mothers 30–40 days of live-in care from an older female relative (mother, mother-in-law, aunt) who baths the baby, cooks, handles chores, and shields the mother from loneliness and PPD. *Source: Punch Nigeria — "Omugwo: How grandmas' care shields new mums from postpartum depression" (healthwise.punchng.com, Oct 2025); The Mindful Birth Group — "Postnatal rituals from around the world" (Nigeria = 30-day omugwo); Momjunction — Omugwo first bath is traditionally done by the grandmother.*

But the diaspora mother in London, Toronto or Houston often has **no omugwo**. A Consultant Psychiatrist cited in Punch notes PPD affects 10–19% globally but **rises to 23% in Nigeria/LMICs** where social support is thin — exactly the diaspora risk. The "firsts" become scarier alone. This is the single biggest commercial wedge in this market.

**2. Every "first" generates documented panic & information overload** (US/UK sources that diaspora mothers also read):
- First night home: Huckleberry survival guide (huckleberrycare.com/blog/first-night-home-with-newborn); Doona; Happiest Baby "nighttime plan" (split shifts, written plan).
- First bath: Mayo Clinic "Baby bath basics" ("nervous about giving your newborn a bath"); Pampers "Your baby's first bath" (sponge baths until cord falls, 7–14 days).
- First doctor visit: TrustCare newborn pediatrician schedule; Nanit; Capital Area Pediatrics; Summit Health (bring a pen, you forget everything).
- First outing: Ergobaby diaper-bag 101; NVmoms first-outing tips; Reddit r/NewParents using Huckleberry to track baths/meds "lifesaver during paediatrician visits."
- First flight: HealthyChildren.org / Consumer Reports / FAA Kids Corner (lap infant, turbulence injuries, ear pressure, TSA PreCheck).
- First illness: Reddit r/NewParents threads on nasal aspirators, "every cry felt like a crisis," "baby sick nonstop."
- First restaurant/public feed: PMC systematic review (PMC9480936) — mothers from **ethnic minority groups report increased surveillance and stigma** breastfeeding in public; UK gov poll — only 57% felt restaurants acceptable; Patient.info — "I walked home to feed him."
- First car trip: SafeInTheSeat (2-hour rule, positional asphyxia); PatPat; Guerin; BabyCentre community (positional asphyxiation fear).
- First birthday: Invyt ("first birthday is really for the parents… a celebration of survival"); Emma's Diary; SMA Baby; Darling Celebrations (budget, overwhelm).
- First holiday: Tommee Tippee; Nest Designs (holiday travel with newborn); Family Traveller (11 tips); Pathways (baby-proofing relative's home with painter's tape).
- First night away / childcare: Calm blog "separation anxiety happens to moms too"; NHS separation anxiety; Park Slope Parents (nanny transition).

**3. Competitor landscape.** Western players sell broad "newborn" books and $20–$40 guides. Nobody sells *single first-time-situation* micro-guides priced at a no-brainer ₦9,800/$9.97, and **nobody** fuses the African omugwo wisdom with diaspora reality. That is our white space.
