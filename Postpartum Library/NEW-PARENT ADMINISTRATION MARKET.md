## RESEARCH BASELINE (real signals gathered via Agent Reach)

**Demand is real and proven — there is NO African/Nigerian-specific product in this space.**
- `familyfolder.com/blog/newborn-paperwork-checklist.html` (read via Jina Reader): "The hard part… is the timing. Not the existence of the paperwork. The timing." Breaks admin into hospital / first-6-weeks / later. Names 8 admin pillars: birth certificate, SSN, health insurance enrolment, paediatrician + medical records, passport, tax, guardianship/will, childcare enrollment. **Competitor exists for US/UK only.**
- `americanheritagecu.org` "Your Newborn Baby Checklist: 10 Administrative Tasks": birth certificate, SSN, insurance, etc. — **US-centric, no African version.**
- `parentingspecialneeds.org` "Drowning in Paperwork": "the administrative duties are an endless, thankless job that barely gets mentioned… the planning and paperwork requirements are next level." Parents use 3-ring binders, accordion folders, dedicated email addresses.
- **Reddit pain (Exa, site:reddit.com):** r/NewParents, r/HuckleberryParents, r/BabyBumps — parents track feeding/diapers/sleep/vaccine appointments in apps (Huckleberry, Nara Baby, Baby Tracker) but complain: "trying a different app… taking too long to learn," "I'd go nuts if I tried to track all his sleep," a husband/wife dev team built a voice-input baby tracker because "every existing app drove me nuts at 3am when I'm barely functional." **Gap: African mothers, printable/offline, partner-shared, culturally framed.**
- **Nigeria birth registration (NPC):** `legalisation.ng` (2026) — NPC birth certificates only for under-17; **child MUST already have a National Identification Number (NIN) before applying**; both parents' NIN slips, marriage cert, hospital birth notification required; portal `birthreg-selfservice.nationalpopulation.gov.ng`; fees ₦1,000–5,000; 2–4 weeks processing. `data.unicef.org/crvs/nigeria` — **registration within 60 days, free, manually processed, requires both parents present.** `idcard.ng` — NIN enrolment fee **$50–$70 USD**.
- **Nigeria maternity leave:** `cxcglobal.com`, `africa-hr.com` — **12 weeks** (private sector, ≥50% pay; public servants +4 weeks); medical certificate required; half-hour nursing breaks on return. `Alive & Thrive` confirms 12 weeks, ≥50% salary.
- **Nigeria NHIS/NHIA:** `quantusmedicalfoundation.com` — NHIA Act 2022 made insurance **mandatory**; covers **children under five + maternity care (up to 4 live births)**; informal sector (traders, artisans, self-employed) can self-enrol via HMO + state schemes (Lagos, Abia, Enugu, Kano); **diaspora Nigerians can fund family coverage in Nigeria.** `bcphr`/`PMC12082677` — only ~5–10% coverage; out-of-pocket >70% of health spend.
- **Nigeria immunization:** `unicef.org/nigeria` Nigeria Immunization Schedule; `jobzilla.ng` 2026 schedule — BCG/OPV0/HepB **at birth**; Pentavalent/PCV/OPV/IPV/Rotavirus at **6, 10, 14 weeks**; needs birth/delivery card to register; card must be kept safe; catch-up plans exist. `pan-ng.org` PAN schedule.
- **Diaspora (UK):** `gov.uk/child-benefit` — claim 48h after birth registration, backdated 3 months, worth **up to £1,248/year**; `nct.org.uk` — SMP needs MAT B1, notify employer by 15th week before due; `newcastle-hospitals.nhs.uk` (2026) — **all new babies get 3 months free NHS, then charges may apply depending on parents' immigration status;** being born in UK ≠ British citizen; visa families must apply to Home Office. `nhsinform.scot` — Best Start Grant/Foods, Scottish Child Payment.
- **Emergency-contact competitors:** Etsy "Baby Emergency Contact Card" ($6.99), `helloplaydate.app` (free wallet card), `theorganizedmomlife.com`, `modernmomlife.com` babysitter info sheet — **all Western, none African/extended-family (nanny, aunty, grandparent) framed.**

**Conclusion:** Huge, validated, underserved. Western products ignore NPC/NIN/NHIS/60-day windows/omugwo gaps/diaspora dual-citizenship. A N9,800/$9.97 printable system is the clear gap.
