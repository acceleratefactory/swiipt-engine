## REAL RESEARCH BASE (Agent Reach — Exa, websearch, Jina/Reddit, academic)
Every niche below is anchored in documented demand. Key evidence:

- **Loneliness is the norm, not the exception.** A 2026 survey of 1,000+ mothers found **73% feel lonely** at least a few times a month and **71% feel disconnected from their old social networks**; half feel lonelier *now* than before children (Women's Agenda, 2026). MAM Baby (UK, 2026) found new mums go an average of **2.4 days without speaking to another adult**, and **1 in 10 went a week or more** without in-person conversation (Netmums). Phoenix Health cites **~82% of new parents experience loneliness**, nearly a third "always or often."
- **Three-dimensional loneliness** (social, emotional, *existential* = "forgotten as a person, only seen as a mother") — Åbo Akademi / Psychreg, 2026. Existential loneliness is the deepest and most overlooked.
- **The village has collapsed structurally.** Smaller families, relatives abroad, no "motherhood apprenticeship," friends without kids drift away (Prof. Tina Miller, Oxford Brookes, via Netmums).
- **African communal care is the antidote — and its absence abroad is the wound.** *Ọmụgwọ/Omugwo* (Igbo), *Wankan Jego* (Hausa), *Ojojo Omo* (Yoruba), *Kuk uman* (Ibibio) = weeks-to-months of live-in grandmother/aunt care that **"curbs loneliness… prevents postpartum depression"** (Punch Healthwise, 2025; BBC Focus on Africa; Guardian Nigeria). Prof. Abubakar Panti (Usmanu Danfodio Univ.) ties the practice directly to lower PPD.
- **Diaspora rupture is documented and painful.** First-Generation Nigerian Mothers (FGNM) in the UK feel **cultural pressure to appear strong**, report a "Neglected Nurturer" experience, and deep **Loneliness** when the omugwo village doesn't materialise (London Metropolitan Univ. IPA study, 2020). Nigerian women birthing abroad describe being **"alone in a foreign land,"** with support absence raising PPD risk (PLoS Global Public Health, 2024). **Grandmother visas get refused** — "parenting in isolation, because immigration said no" (OLADAM, 2025). Families are **"crowdsourcing Omugwo" via WhatsApp, church meal-trains, prayer circles** (OLADAM).
- **Church is both refuge and site of judgment.** Devout mums have been **asked to leave Mass / service because a baby cried** (BBC NI; Catholic All Year; Sydney Anglicans on "cry-room anxiety"). Yet church community is repeatedly named as the *replacement family* for diaspora Nigerians (IJNM London study; Sage "Dialling Up/Down").
- **Leaving the house is a genuine phobia.** "You have been staring at the front door for twelve minutes… the most terrifying thing you have attempted" (BubsNest, 2026). First solo outings provoke panic; over-packing, fear of public nappy changes and feeding (BubsNest; AsiaOne).
- **Restaurants & travel are "races against the clock"** with meltdown risk (Parent.com; Solid Starts; Happiest Baby; HealthyChildren/Scripps flying-with-baby).
- **In-law / family visits trigger boundary wars & unsolicited advice** (InfantCareBlog; Phoenix Health; Colief: 70% of parents get unhelpful unsolicited advice).
- **Making mom-friends is "like dating"** — repeated misses before a match (JessKeys; FIT4MOM; Mother.ly; Peanut app).
- **Refugee/immigrant African mothers** face displacement, language isolation, loss of traditional 40-day care (BMC Pregnancy; Black African Newcomer Women, Canada — rely on "mental strength, spirituality, spousal support").
- **Reddit pain threads** confirm: SAHM overwhelmed/isolated (r/stayathomemoms), boundary-crossing family (r/workingmoms), breastfeeding despair (r/BabyBumps) — the silence is global.
