Every product in this library includes a **professional‑help signpost**: a visible "When to see a doctor/counsellor now" page (red‑flag symptoms: thoughts of harming self or baby, inability to function, psychosis) pointing to local Nigerian services (e.g., psychiatrist via teaching hospitals, Lagos/Centre for Mental Health, *988‑style* crisis lines where available) and diaspora resources (NHS perinatal mental health, Postpartum Support International, SAMHSA).
