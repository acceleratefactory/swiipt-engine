[[DECISION]]
TITLE: The Stump Decision Tree
SUB: Run this every time the stump looks "wrong." One question at a time. No guessing.
START: START — What changed?
ROUTE: emergency | Baby unwell? Fever 38C / 100.4F or higher (or feels hot), unusually cold body, floppy / hard to wake, refusing feeds, inconsolable cry | EMERGENCY NOW — go to hospital today | Do not apply anything to the stump. Do not wait for morning.
ROUTE: urgent | Skin AROUND the stump (not the stump itself) red or spreading, swollen, hard, hot — or baby cries when that skin is touched | URGENT TODAY — clinic or hospital today | Newborn infections move fast, even if baby seems fine.
ROUTE: urgent | Thick yellow / green / white fluid (pus) from the base, or a foul / rotting smell (not the mild musk of separation) | Same-day medical care | Infection suspected.
ROUTE: urgent | Active bleeding beyond a few drops — still bleeding after 10 minutes of gentle pressure | Care now | Apply gentle pressure, then go.
ROUTE: ok | Dark, dry, hard, shrinking stump; slight musty smell; small dried blood spot near separation | Normal drying — continue the routine | Log it in the tracker.
ROUTE: ok | Pink moist lump after separation (likely granuloma), or stump still attached after 21 days | Book a clinic visit this week | Not an emergency, but needs a review.
BAND: Educational triage support — never a diagnosis. When unsure, present to care.
[[/DECISION]]

## If someone applied something to the stump

1. Gently clean the area with plain water on a clean cloth. Pat dry.
2. Photograph the stump now and again in 12 hours.
3. Run the decision tree above again.
4. Use the reset script with the person (see Script Pack). No blame — just the rule.
5. Log it in the tracker and watch extra closely for 48 hours.

> ## Memorize the three questions
> **Is the baby unwell?** — Emergency. **Is the skin around the stump red, swollen or hot?** — Today. **Is there pus or a foul smell?** — Today. Anything else: continue the routine and re-check in 12 hours.
