TITLE: BAD NIGHT PROTOCOL + WHEN EXHAUSTION NEEDS MORE
*If you are reading this at 3am because everything broke - start here.*

## When a night collapses
1. Default to whoever's turn it technically is.
2. The override triggers automatic next-night compensation (log it in the tracker).
3. Do not renegotiate the whole system at 3am. Just run the fallback.

## When the system keeps breaking
- Missed shift -> no-blame reset script; adjust the alarm/handoff method.
- Refusal -> use the "trial it for one week" framing from the Script Pack.
- Overwhelm -> simplify to ONE non-negotiable block; drop the rest temporarily.
- Relapse -> restart at the Negotiate step; no guilt.
- New helper arrives with old advice -> they read the shift chart before a night shift.

## When exhaustion needs more than a night plan
**This card cannot and does not diagnose anything.** Stop planning if you notice:
- hopelessness or persistent low mood beyond ordinary tiredness
- intrusive thoughts
- being unable to function during the day

Do not wait. Use the verified shared crisis/escalation list (Product Pipeline/MEMORY.md) - the real, verified numbers for Nigeria and the diaspora. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number now.
