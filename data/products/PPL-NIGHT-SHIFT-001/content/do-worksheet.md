## Safety guardrails - both adults read and agree before you sign

These are not optional. They are the boundary the whole system runs inside.

**1. Infant safety is paramount.** This contract controls adult coordination only - it does NOT override safe-sleep practice. The baby always sleeps on their back, on a firm, flat, separate surface (crib or bassinet), free of blankets, pillows, bumper pads, or soft toys, in line with World Health Organization (WHO) and paediatric guidance. If you are unsure what safe sleep looks like for your baby's age and stage, ask your paediatrician or midwife before you start a roster.

**2. The active shift manager stays fully awake.** Whoever is on shift must remain awake and alert while handling the baby. Never hold the baby in a bed, sofa, or armchair if there is any risk of falling asleep. If you feel unsafe from fatigue, use the emergency handoff immediately (see the Rescue Card).

**3. No earplugs or sleep aids on shift.** The parent on shift must NOT wear earplugs or headphones, or use any sleep-inducing aid - they must be able to hear and respond to the baby. The off-duty parent MAY use earplugs or white noise to protect their sleep - that is the point of the system.

**4. The baby always overrides the roster.** If the baby cannot be settled, or you are worried about feeding, illness, injury, breathing, or any unusual behaviour, assess the baby and follow your existing paediatric or clinical guidance, or seek help. Use the agreed phrase **"I need a handoff."** The roster pauses. Baby comes first.
