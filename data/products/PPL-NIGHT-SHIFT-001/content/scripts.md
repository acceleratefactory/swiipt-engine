[[SCRIPTS]]
TITLE: Seven Script Cards
SUB: Ready words for the seven hardest moments. Calm, firm, no war.
SCRIPT: Opening the conversation (not a complaint) | I've been running every night on no sleep, and I don't want us to keep re-fighting this at 3am. I want us to agree a simple roster, tonight. I'll show you the two options - can we pick one before bed?
SCRIPT: "I have work tomorrow" | I know work is real. So is the fact that I've had no 3-hour block in weeks. This roster protects both of us: your work nights are planned, and I get one protected block. Let's run it for one week and review.
SCRIPT: "I don't hear the baby" | That's fair - you sleep deeply. So we don't rely on hearing. Your shift has an alarm, and I hand the baby over at a fixed time so it doesn't depend on you waking up.
SCRIPT: "You're breastfeeding, what can I do?" | You can take the resettles and nappies and hand her to me only for feeds, then put her back. That way my block is real even though I'm feeding.
SCRIPT: "My mother says this is your job" | I respect your mother. Our baby, our home, our roster. We're not asking her to run our nights.
SCRIPT: It broke last night (reset, no blame) | Last night didn't go to plan. That's a data point, not a failure. Let's just fix the alarm/handoff for tonight and keep going.
SCRIPT: When I feel the rage rise | I am exhausted, not broken. The roster exists so this doesn't land on him sideways. Tonight is his shift; I will sleep. If this feeling is more than tiredness, I will use the rescue card.
BAND: The pattern: honour the person -> state the rule once -> give them a role. You are not arguing; you are leading.
[[/SCRIPTS]]
