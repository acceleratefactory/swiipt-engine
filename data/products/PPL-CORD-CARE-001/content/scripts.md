# Script Pack
*Seven ready words for the seven hardest moments. Warm, firm, zero war.*

## Script 1 — Announcing the rule to family (send BEFORE the baby arrives or Day 1)

> "We've read the current medical guidance and made one house rule for the cord: nothing goes on it — clean, dry, exposed. We know everyone wants the best for the baby, and this is our way of protecting her. Thank you for respecting it."

## Script 2 — Grandma offers shea butter / toothpaste / herbs

> "Mama, I know this is what you used and you raised wonderful children with it. The doctors now say the stump heals best when nothing touches it. Your help means everything to me — let's use your strength on [another task] instead."

*(Redirect the care, don't reject the carer.)*

## Script 3 — Someone did it anyway (the no-blame reset, private)

> "I know you did it out of love. We cleaned it off and the baby is fine. But we agreed nothing goes on the stump — if we're not sure about something, ask me first and we'll check together. Deal?"

## Script 4 — "In our day they used hot compress and nothing happened"

> "You're right that many babies were fine — and some weren't, that's the part nobody talks about. The new guidance is simple: dry heals fastest. Let's do what keeps THIS one safest."

## Script 5 — Partner won't back you up

> "I need one thing from you, not agreement with everything my mother says: when cord questions come up, your answer is 'we follow our house rule.' We can discuss anything else privately. In front of family, we're one voice."

## Script 6 — Telling work/friends why you're not travelling to the naming ceremony yet

> "Baby's stump hasn't healed enough for us to move around yet. Our paediatrician wants us to keep it clean and exposed until separation. We'll celebrate properly the moment it's safe — you'll get the first photos!"

## Script 7 — When YOU feel the fear rise at midnight

> "Stump looks strange. Baby is feeding/sleeping/temperature normal. Skin around it is normal colour. No pus, no bad smell. This is worry, not warning. Decision tree says: re-check in 12 hours. If anything changes, we go in."

---

**The pattern in every script:** honour the person → state the rule once → give them a role. You are never arguing; you are leading.
