# The Stump Decision Tree
*Run this every time the stump looks "wrong." One question at a time. No guessing.*

## START: What changed?

### Branch 1 — Is the baby unwell?

**Does the baby have ANY of these?**
- Fever 38°C/100.4°F or higher (or feels hot), OR unusually cold body
- Very sleepy, floppy, hard to wake for feeds
- Refusing feeds or feeding far less than normal
- Inconsolable crying unlike normal hunger cries

→ **YES to any: EMERGENCY NOW.** Go to hospital today. Do not apply anything to the stump. Do not wait for morning.

→ **NO to all:** go to Branch 2.

---

### Branch 2 — The stump itself

**Look at the SKIN AROUND the stump (not the stump):**

→ **Redness spreading outward onto the belly, or skin swollen/hard/hot, or baby cries when that skin is touched**
= **URGENT TODAY.** Clinic or hospital today, even if baby seems fine. Newborn infections move fast.

→ **Skin around the stump is normal colour**
Go to Branch 3.

---

### Branch 3 — The stump surface

**What do you see on the stump itself?**

| Finding | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Dark, dry, hard, shrinking | Normal drying | Continue routine |
| Slight musty smell | Normal near separation | Continue routine |
| Small dried blood spot | Normal at separation | Continue; log it |
| Thick yellow/green/white fluid (pus) from base | **Infection suspected** | **Same-day medical care** |
| Foul/rotting smell (not mild musk) | **Infection suspected** | **Same-day medical care** |
| Active bleeding beyond a few drops | Apply gentle pressure 10 min → still bleeding = **care now** | Care now |
| Pink moist lump after separation | Likely granuloma (not emergency) | **Clinic visit this week** |
| Still attached after 21 days | Delayed separation | **Book review this week** |

---

### Branch 4 — Someone applied something

If a substance was applied (by anyone):
1. Gently clean the area with plain water on a clean cloth. Pat dry.
2. Photograph the stump now and in 12 hours.
3. Run Branches 1–3 above.
4. Use the reset script with the person (see Script Pack). No blame — just the rule.
5. Log it in the tracker. Watch extra closely for 48 hours.

---

## The three-question summary (memorize this)

1. **Is the baby unwell?** → Emergency.
2. **Is the skin around the stump red/swollen/hot?** → Today.
3. **Is there pus or foul smell?** → Today.

Anything else: continue the routine and re-check in 12 hours.

*Educational triage support — never a diagnosis. When unsure, present to care.*
