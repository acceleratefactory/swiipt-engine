TITLE: RED FLAG RESCUE CARD
*If you are looking at this card because something scared you - start here. Do not pass go.*

## GO TO HOSPITAL NOW (emergency, today, right now)

**ANY ONE of these:**

- **Fever 38C / 100.4F or higher** - OR baby feels abnormally COLD, floppy, and hard to wake *(newborns can crash the OTHER way - cold is as dangerous as hot)*
- **Redness spreading outward from the stump onto the belly skin**
- **Thick yellow/green/white pus from the stump**, especially with a foul/rotting smell
- **Skin around the stump swollen, hard, hot, or baby screams when it's touched**
- **Refusing feeds + unusually sleepy + just "not right"**
- **Bleeding that has not stopped after 10 minutes of gentle pressure**

### While getting help:
- Do NOT apply anything to the stump (no spirit, no ointment, nothing)
- Keep baby warm but not overheated
- Bring your tracker log - it tells the doctor the timeline

## CALL/CLINIC TODAY (urgent, within hours)

- Mild redness right at the base without spreading AND baby completely well -> still get seen today; photograph it hourly until you're there
- You suspect someone applied a substance and can't confirm what it was

## BOOK THIS WEEK (not emergency)

- Stump still attached at 21 days
- After separation: continuous oozing beyond 2-3 days
- Pink moist lump at the navel (granuloma - simple clinic treatment)

## NONE of the above?

You are in worry, not warning. Decision tree -> continue routine -> re-check in 12 hours. Photo now for comparison.
