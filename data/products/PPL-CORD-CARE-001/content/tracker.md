## Milestones (normal ranges)

- Days 1-3: clamp on/off, stump moist-dark
- Days 5-15: drying, hardening, separation typically happens here
- After separation: small raw spot heals within days; navel fully healed by ~week 3
- Day 21 with stump attached -> book review
