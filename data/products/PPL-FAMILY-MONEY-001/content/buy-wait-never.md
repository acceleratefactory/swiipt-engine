# Buy / Wait / Never List
*Every intended purchase gets one of three verdicts before any money leaves the household. Nothing stays undecided.*

## The one question per purchase
Ask it in this order and stop at the first answer that fits.

| Verdict | Ask this | If the answer is yes | If the answer is no |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Buy** | Does the baby need this now, and is it already inside the normal-month figure? | Buy it. Cross it off. | Go to Wait. |
| **Wait** | Is this wanted but not yet proven necessary? | Write it on the Wait row with the date you will re-check it. Do not buy yet. | Go to Never. |
| **Never** | Does this solve a problem you do not have, or replace something you already own and use? | Take it out of the cart. It is not a saving to buy it "just in case". | It is a genuine Wait or Buy — re-read the two lines above. |

> ## Two rules that stop the loop
> **1. Essentials are never "Never".** Feeding, warmth, safety and health basics are not up for debate. Never is for the unnecessary, not the uncomfortable.
> **2. If you are not sure, it is Wait.** Wait is not a delay tactic. It has a date on it, and on that date you decide again with better information.

## How to run it
1. Fill the verdict column for everything currently in a watchlist, cart or saved list. Do not buy while filling.
2. For every Wait, write a re-check date. A Wait without a date becomes a slow Buy.
3. For every Never, remove it now, while you are calm and the fear is not driving.
4. Move a Wait to Buy only when the need is real and the money is inside the plan.

## Example (illustrative only — your verdicts will differ)
| Item | Verdict | Wait until | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extra set of night feed bottles | Wait | 2 weeks | One set is working; a second is convenience until proven needed |
| Second pram for the car | Never | — | One pram already covers both uses |
| Larger sleep sack for the current size | Buy | — | The current one no longer fits; safety and comfort need it now |

These rows show the shape of a decision. They are not advice about those items.

## What this list is not
It classifies a purchase against your own plan. It does not tell you what you can afford, and it is not regulated financial advice, debt counselling or investment advice. If an essential genuinely does not fit the plan, that is a plan problem — take it to the One-Number Budget Chart and the audit, not to this list alone.
