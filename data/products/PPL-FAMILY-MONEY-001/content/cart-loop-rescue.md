TITLE: Cart-Delete Loop Rescue
## What is happening
You load a cart, feel the number, delete everything, and re-add it a day later. Nothing is decided. The fear is doing the buying, not the plan. This is the failure the system exists to catch, and it has a fixed way out.

## Minimum Mode — the only three moves
Do these three, in order, and nothing else tonight.

1. Post the chart again. Read the normal-month figure out loud, even to yourself. The number still exists.
2. Take whatever is in the cart right now and put every item through the Buy / Wait / Never List. One verdict each. Essentials are not allowed to be "Never".
3. Re-open the audit log and add today's date as the restart line. You are back in the system, not outside it.

## The 20-minute rule
If you cannot settle it in twenty minutes, it is a **Wait**, not a Buy. Write the date you will re-check it. Go to sleep. A Wait costs nothing.

## After a broken week
A missed week is data, not a verdict on you. Do not restart the whole first-year plan. Restart only the current cycle from the chart, and only re-run the quiz if your income or your household changed.

## Where this system ends
This is household money organisation and decision support. It is **not** regulated financial advice, debt counselling, credit advice, investment advice, therapy, relationship counselling, or emergency financial assistance. It does not promise savings and it cannot tell you what you can afford.

If your situation has moved beyond ordinary planning — for example, you are behind on essential bills, facing pressure to borrow you cannot repay, or the money stress is affecting your health or your safety — this system is the wrong tool for that part, and the plan pauses there. Speak to a qualified, regulated professional in your own country (a licensed money adviser, debt adviser, or your bank's hardship team) and, where the strain is on your health or safety, a qualified health professional. Do not rely on a household worksheet for a situation that needs a regulated professional.

No emergency contact numbers are printed here, because this product is not a crisis service and no financial-crisis service is recorded as approved for it. Use the services that are already verified in your own country.

## One line to remember
The system does not fail when a week is broken. It fails only when the chart is never posted again.
