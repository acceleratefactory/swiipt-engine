# The No-Village C-Section
*First 14 days alone with a newborn after major abdominal surgery - survival and function, no village required.*

## Who this is for

You came home from a C-section without a mother, auntie, or cousin installed in your guest room. Maybe your partner got two weeks off, maybe five days, maybe none. Everyone online says "accept help" - but there is no help standing in your kitchen. This guide assumes **nobody is coming**, and builds your first 14 days anyway.

## The one fact that organizes everything

A C-section is major abdominal surgery. You are a surgical patient carrying a newborn. Every instruction below flows from that: protect the incision, move gently but move, and lower the bar for everything that is not baby + healing.

## Your 14-day map

**Days 1–3 (survive):** pain on schedule, not on courage. Walk short loops. Everything within arm's reach.
**Days 4–7 (function):** shower normally, short walks outside, one "win task" per day max.
**Days 8–14 (rhythm):** light routine returns; still nothing heavier than the baby.

## Pain management (the honest version)

- Paracetamol (acetaminophen) and ibuprofen are the standard pair and are considered compatible with breastfeeding - take them ON SCHEDULE the first week, before pain spikes, exactly as your surgeon prescribed.
- Pain that arrives late "because I'm tough" costs you sleep, mobility, and healing.
- A pillow pressed to your belly when coughing, sneezing, laughing, or rolling changes everything.

## Moving safely

- Rolling out of bed: drop legs first, push up with arms ("log roll").
- Walk every few hours, even just kitchen-to-sofa - walking prevents clots.
- Nothing heavier than the baby for ~2 weeks; nothing heavier than ~10–15 lb / 6 kg (roughly a full shopping bag) until your 6-week review says otherwise.
- No driving until you can brake hard without hesitating (commonly around 2 weeks; your clinician decides).
- No baths/swimming until your clinician confirms healed (showers fine).

## Incision care

Shower water over it is fine; mild soap, pat dry, no scrubbing, no creams/pastes/herbs on it. Air + time heals. Watch it daily with the Decision Card.

## Feeding the machine (you)

You heal on food and water, not on leftovers. One-handed meals count. Protein + water at every chance. If appetite vanished: small, often.

## The no-village systems

1. **The Landing Zone:** one chair/bed corner with 48 hours of supplies - water bottle, snacks, chargers, nappies, wipes, muslins. Baby changes happen there.
2. **The One-Trip Rule:** never climb stairs or cross the house for one item. Carry a basket.
3. **The 15-Minute Visitor:** anyone who visits brings food or holds the baby WHILE YOU SHOWER - then goes home. Script Pack has the words.
4. **Delivery apps > hero cooking.** This is the two weeks it's worth it.

## What "normal" feels like

Sore incision improving daily, bleeding like a heavy period fading over weeks, afterpains while breastfeeding, exhaustion that sleep partially fixes, weepy "baby blues" easing after two weeks. None of that is failure.

*Sources: Mayo Clinic C-section recovery guidance; Cleveland Clinic recovery timeline (2021); WebMD post-C-section recovery & warning signs; Medical News Today wound-infection review. Educational only - your own clinician overrides this guide.*

# Daily Survival Checklist (Days 1–14)
*One tick list. Survive first, function second.*

## Morning (10 minutes)
- [ ] Pain meds taken (on schedule, not on pain)
- [ ] Water bottle filled - it lives next to you now
- [ ] Incision peeked at (photo every 2 days)
- [ ] Today has ONE goal only. Name it: ____________

## Movement (every few hours)
- [ ] Walked a loop (bed → kitchen counts on Days 1–3)
- [ ] Did the log-roll getting up, pillow-hug when sneezing/coughing
- [ ] Stretched shoulders/breathes: 3 deep breaths, 10 shoulder rolls

## Lifting rules (non-negotiable)
- [ ] Lifted nothing heavier than the baby
- [ ] Toddler/older child: sit-down cuddles only, no lifting
- [ ] Asked for/delivered help with anything heavier

## Evening reset (15 minutes)
- [ ] Tomorrow's Landing Zone restocked: water, snacks, nappies, wipes, charger
- [ ] Fed yourself actual food today? If not: note why, fix tomorrow
- [ ] One-line log: pain __/10 · walked? Y/N · incision normal? Y/N

## Week 2 additions (Days 8–14)
- [ ] One short outdoor walk daily
- [ ] Booked/attended the ~2-week postnatal check
- [ ] Practiced the brake-test question if driving is near: can I slam the brakes without thinking twice?
