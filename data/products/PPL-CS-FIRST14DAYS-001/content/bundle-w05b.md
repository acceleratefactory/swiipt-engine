# The Incision Decision Card
*Look at the incision + how you feel. One pass. Act on what you find.*

## 1 · Is ANY of these true right now?

- Fever **38°C / 100.4°F or higher**, or chills/shivering
- Incision: red spreading outward, hot, swollen, opening, or leaking pus / bad-smelling fluid
- Pain that is **getting worse** instead of better after day 3–4
- Vaginal bleeding soaking a pad per hour, large clots, or foul smell
- Calf pain/swelling/heat on one leg, chest pain, or shortness of breath
- Severe headache that will not shift, or vision changes

→ **ANY ONE = contact your clinician TODAY. Fever + any incision sign = same hour. Chest pain/breathlessness/calf signs = emergency now (clot until proven otherwise).**

## 2 · None of those, but worried about looks?

| Seeing | Meaning | Do |
|---|---|---|
| Slight redness thin line, mild swelling days 1–3 | Normal healing | Routine continues |
| Pink/purple fading scar line weeks later | Normal maturing | Routine continues |
| Small clear fluid seep after activity | Borderline | Photo today + tomorrow; call clinic if it repeats/worsens |
| Hard lump at one end | Watch | Mention at week-2 check; photo log |
| Weeping past day 5 / reopening edges | Not normal | Clinic today |
| Numbness around incision | Very common | Normal for months |

## 3 · The mood check (30 seconds)

Beyond two weeks of weepy blues → or any moment of "I can't do this," hopelessness, thoughts of harming yourself or the baby → **this is medical, not moral.** Contact your clinician and use the crisis lines on your Rescue Card. Screens route; they never diagnose.

## 4 · Someone touched the incision / applied something

Herbs, pastes, belly-binding over an unhealed wound: rinse gently with clean water if freshly applied, photograph, run section 1, use the reset script, log it.

# No-Village Scripts
*You are not superwoman and you don't owe anyone the performance of coping.*

## Script 1 — The visitor who sits down expecting tea

> "So glad you came - you're actually perfect timing. Hold him while I grab a shower, then I'll walk you out, I'm on painkillers schedule."

*(They held the baby. You showered. Fifteen minutes, dignity intact.)*

## Script 2 — Declining the trip/event in week 2

> "Recovery from the surgery isn't there yet - doctor says no long trips this fortnight. We'll be there properly once I'm cleared at my check-up."

## Script 3 — Asking for REAL help (specific asks work)

> "If you want to help us this week, here's exactly what would change our lives: Tuesday groceries, Wednesday laundry pickup, Friday a pot of soup. Pick one?"

## Script 4 — Partner got five paternity days and is back at work

> "We need to plan your week like a rota: nights are split, mornings you handle [task] before leaving, weekends we trade shifts so I sleep in. This is two more weeks, then we renegotiate."

## Script 5 — Long-distance village (the diaspora special)

> Group chat message: "We're doing meal-drop deliveries instead of visits: Tue/Thu/Sun slots open. Address to send courier: [x]. Thank you - this is the village, remote edition."

## Script 6 — "My mother would have come if she could" grief moment

> Say it out loud to yourself: *"She couldn't come. I am allowed to find my own way. Look what I did today anyway."*

## Script 7 — When baby blues turn dark (say this to someone)

> "I'm not okay. This feels beyond tired. Can you come sit with me / can I call you tonight?" - and book the clinician line. You would never judge a friend for this.

# Bad-Day Rescue Card
*For the day everything goes wrong. It happens. Here's the reset.*

## The rule of the bad day

A bad day is not a failed recovery. Surgical healing is not linear. Today we drop to MINIMUM MODE and tomorrow restarts - no debt, no guilt.

## Minimum Mode (the only 5 things)

1. Meds on schedule
2. Baby fed somehow (breast/bottle/expressed - all count)
3. You ate ONE real thing and drank water
4. Incision glance + red-flag scan
5. Everyone alive → day counted as survived ✓

Everything else - laundry, visitors, the perfect routine - waits.

## Common bad days → instant moves

**"I overdid it and I'm wrecked"** → Cancel tomorrow's goal. Meds, ice-pack comfort, horizontal. Healing happens lying down.

**"Incision popped a sting / bled a dot after lifting"** → Photo, pressure 10 min, Decision Card scan. No repeat lift. Call clinic if reopened/oozing.

**"Nobody checked on me today"** → Send Script 5 to the group chat NOW. State the specific ask. People genuinely don't know.

**"I cried all afternoon"** → Hydrate, eat, tell one human by voice/text. Two weeks+ of this = clinician call. Crisis numbers below.

**"I resent the baby" (fleeting horror thought)** → Extremely common, not a verdict on you. Sleep-deprived brains say wild things. If persistent/darkening → clinician, today-tomorrow.
