# Beyond the Restroom: 30-Day Return-to-Work OS
*The 30 days before and the 30 days after maternity leave ends - planned like a project, run like a system.*

## Why this exists

"Going back to work" advice is either corporate HR boilerplate or vague "self-care." Meanwhile the actual problems - pumping at work, the morning hand-off, supply logistics, guilt math, the 5pm scramble - hit in week one with no plan. This is that plan: **15 days before, 15 days after**, each day one small move.

## Know your rights first (this changes your negotiation)

**United States:** The PUMP Act (2022) requires nearly all employers to provide reasonable break time plus a private, non-bathroom space to pump for one year after birth - pumping time counts as work time when not fully relieved. The Pregnant Workers Fairness Act adds accommodation rights.

**United Kingdom:** No statutory pumping-break law, but the Equality Act 2010 treats unfavourable treatment of breastfeeding as sex discrimination, and HSE guidance says employers should provide a clean private space (never toilets). 52 weeks' statutory maternity leave; flexible-working requests are a formal process.

**Nigeria:** The Labour Act floor is twelve weeks' maternity leave at no less than 50% pay (section 54) - verify your employer's contract/policy which often exceeds this; nursing breaks are commonly negotiated case by case in practice.

Wherever you are: **your ask is an adjustment request backed by law/guidance, not a favour.**

## Phase 1 - BEFORE (Days -15 to -1): build the machine

- **D-15:** Confirm return date + schedule in writing. Choose your feeding strategy (Decision Card).
- **D-14 to -10:** Build the freezer bank: +2 bags/day beyond baby's needs. Rehearse one full separation morning.
- **D-9:** Childcare locked + one backup named. Paid trial half-day if possible.
- **D-8:** The Employer Ask (Script Pack) - pump space/time or flexibility request, in writing.
- **D-7 to -3:** Run the Morning Drill twice: full route, real timing, note friction.
- **D-2:** Pack the Work Bag: pump, parts, cooler, labels, spare top, snacks, charger, photos of baby (for letdown).
- **D-1:** Night-before ritual: bags by door, bottles prepped, clothes laid out, alarm = baby's feed.

## Phase 2 - AFTER (Days +1 to +15): run the system

**The three anchors that hold everything:**
1. **Morning Hand-off:** same person, same spot, same 4 lines every workday ("last feed ___ml, nappies ___, mood ___").
2. **Pump Blocks:** calendar-blocked, non-negotiable, ~every 3 hours. Skipping blocks = supply tax + pain today.
3. **The 5pm Bridge:** evening feed/reconnect ritual that resets you both - phone away first 20 minutes.

**Weekly rhythm:** Sunday 20-minute reset (meals, bag restock, laundry loop). Wednesday mid-week check: what broke? Fix ONE thing.

## The guilt chapter (read twice)

Guilt spikes when values collide unexamined. Write yours down: providing income IS care. A regulated, fulfilled mother is a gift, not a betrayal. Your baby does not need a martyr; they need you thriving on both sides of 5pm. If sadness becomes persistent hopelessness - that's clinical territory, use the Rescue lines.

## Supply & storage quick rules

Wash hands . store milk in clean sealed containers . refrigerate up to ~4 days / freeze best within 3 months . label date . thaw oldest-first in fridge, never microwave . swirl, don't shake hard. When unsure about a batch: smell, then bin if off - never re-feed leftover from a finished feed.

*Sources: US DOL/EEOC PUMP Act + PWFA materials (2024); CDC workplace breastfeeding guidance; UK Maternity Action/NHS/HSE guidance; Nigeria Labour Act s54. Educational only.*

# Feeding-at-Work Decision Card
*Pick your lane before Day 1. All four are legitimate wins.*

## Start here

**Can you get to baby at lunch?**
-> YES: **Lunch-reunion lane.** Express mid-morning only if engorged; nurse at lunch; evenings normal. Simplest hardware.

-> NO: continue.

**Do you WANT to keep breast milk as the main milk?**
-> YES: **Pumping lane.** Blocks every ~3h; storage kit; freezer bank covers Monday chaos.

-> It doesn't fit your job/body reality:
-> **Combo lane.** Breast at home mornings/nights + formula at childcare. Zero shame - fed, loved, employed. Caregiver preps per tin instructions exactly; never dilute.

-> Circumstances say exclusive formula: **Formula lane.** Your hands stay free of pump parts and your lunch break is actually yours. Same love, different container.

## Any-lane logistics check

- Where will milk/formula live at childcare? Labelled? Cold chain?
- Who thaws/preps? Written instructions given?
- Backup feed in their cupboard Day 1?

## If the employer pushback happens

Re-read your rights block in the Guide. Respond in writing: "Happy to solve logistics together - my request matches [PUMP Act 2022 / HSE guidance / company policy]." Escalation path: HR written -> ACAS/Maternity Action (UK) -> DOL complaint (US) -> union/labour officer (NG).

## Mood guardrail

If feeding-at-work becomes daily dread or grief spirals: that's data, not weakness. Lactation consultant (lane change is allowed!) + crisis numbers. Fed-and-well beats any badge.

TITLE: BEYOND THE RESTROOM - BAD-DAY RESCUE CARD
*If you are opening this because today went wrong - you are not failing. You are using the system's built-in recovery mode.*

## The rule of the bad day
A bad day does not erase your system. It activates **Minimum Mode**. You do not "make up" for a lost day. You run the smaller system and continue.

## MINIMUM MODE - the only 5 things
On a bad day, do ONLY these:
1. **Baby fed** (however feeding happens today)
2. **Pump or nurse at your longest work gap** - even 10 minutes protects supply
3. **Bags in the freezer** - whatever you get is banked, zero guilt about quantity
4. **One line to your childcare person** - what time, what pickup, nothing else
5. **Sleep when the baby sleeps tonight** - dishes wait

Everything else on the checklist can slide ONE day without damage.

## Common bad days - instant moves

### Supply suddenly dropped
- Check: new bra? period returning? skipped two pumps? dehydration?
- Add ONE extra session tonight (even 8 minutes signals "make more")
- Water bottle next to every pump site starting now
- Judge supply at DAY level, never session level

### Employer blocked / squeezed your pump breaks
- Do not negotiate live. Say: *"I'll check my notes and come back to you this afternoon."*
- Send Script 2 (the pushback reply) from a calm hour, not the hallway
- Log it: date, time, what was said. Patterns matter; incidents don't.

### Childcare collapsed this morning
- Call the backup name on your Day-0 card BEFORE panicking
- If no backup: use the emergency-day protocol you wrote in Week -1 (who covers, for how long)
- Text your lead: *"Family emergency this morning - online by [time]."* Short is professional.

### Pain, fever, hot red streak on breast
- Fever + flu feeling + hot spot = possible mastitis. **Call your clinic TODAY.**
- Keep milk moving (feed/pump that side) on the way to the call
- This is common, treatable, and not a failure of the system

### The night-before disaster (nothing prepped)
- Run Minimum Mode tomorrow. Prep ONE thing tonight (bags labeled OR outfit out) - not all of it.

## The mood guardrail
If you have cried most of the day for 3+ days, or you feel numb/dark/not-yourself:
- Tell one human TODAY - partner, sister, friend, the Script 7 line
- Postpartum mood conditions are medical, not moral. Treatment works.
