# Beyond the Restroom: 30-Day Return-to-Work OS
*The 30 days before and the 30 days after maternity leave ends - planned like a project, run like a system.*

## Why this exists

"Going back to work" advice is either corporate HR boilerplate or vague "self-care." Meanwhile the actual problems - pumping at work, the morning hand-off, supply logistics, guilt math, the 5pm scramble - hit in week one with no plan. This is that plan: **15 days before, 15 days after**, each day one small move.

## Know your rights first (this changes your negotiation)

**United States:** The PUMP Act (2022) requires nearly all employers to provide reasonable break time plus a private, non-bathroom space to pump for one year after birth - pumping time counts as work time when not fully relieved. The Pregnant Workers Fairness Act adds accommodation rights.

**United Kingdom:** No statutory pumping-break law, but the Equality Act 2010 treats unfavourable treatment of breastfeeding as sex discrimination, and HSE guidance says employers should provide a clean private space (never toilets). 52 weeks' statutory maternity leave exists; flexible-working requests are a formal process.

**Nigeria:** The Labour Act floor is twelve weeks' maternity leave at no less than 50% pay (§54) - verify your employer's contract/policy which often exceeds this; nursing breaks are commonly negotiated case by case in practice.

Wherever you are: **your ask is an adjustment request backed by law/guidance, not a favour.**

## Phase 1 · BEFORE (Days -15 to -1): build the machine

- **D-15:** Confirm return date + schedule in writing. Choose your feeding strategy for work (see Decision Card).
- **D-14 to -10:** Build the freezer bank: +2 bags/day beyond baby's needs. Rehearse one full separation morning.
- **D-9:** Childcare locked + one backup named. Do a paid trial half-day if possible.
- **D-8:** The Employer Ask (Script Pack) - pump space/time or flexibility request, in writing.
- **D-7 to -3:** Run the Morning Drill twice: full route, real timing, note friction.
- **D-2:** Pack the Work Bag: pump, parts, cooler, labels, spare top, snacks, charger, photos of baby (for letdown).
- **D-1:** Night-before ritual: bags by door, bottles prepped, clothes laid out, alarm = baby's feed.

## Phase 2 · AFTER (Days +1 to +15): run the system

**The three anchors that hold everything:**
1. **Morning Hand-off:** same person, same spot, same 4 lines every workday ("last feed ___ml, nappies ___, mood ___").
2. **Pump Blocks:** calendar-blocked, non-negotiable, ~every 3 hours. Skipping blocks = supply tax + pain today.
3. **The 5pm Bridge:** evening feed/reconnect ritual that resets you both - phone away first 20 minutes.

**Weekly rhythm:** Sunday 20-minute reset (meals, bag restock, laundry loop). Wednesday mid-week check: what broke? Fix ONE thing.

## The guilt chapter (read twice)

Guilt spikes when values collide unexamined. Write yours down: providing income IS care. A regulated, fulfilled mother is a gift, not a betrayal. Your baby does not need a martyr; they need you thriving on both sides of 5pm. If sadness becomes persistent hopelessness - that's clinical territory, use the Rescue Card lines.

## Supply & storage quick rules

Wash hands · store milk in clean sealed containers · refrigerate up to ~4 days / freeze ~6 months (best by 3) · label date · thaw oldest-first in fridge, never microwave · swirl, don't shake hard. When unsure about a batch: smell, then bin if off - never re-feed leftover from a finished feed.

# Feeding-at-Work Decision Card
*Pick your lane before Day 1. All four are legitimate wins.*

## Start here

**Can you get to baby at lunch?**
→ YES: **Lunch-reunion lane.** Pump/express mid-morning only if engorged; nurse at lunch; evenings normal. Simplest hardware.

→ NO: continue.

**Do you WANT to keep breast milk as the main milk?**
→ YES: **Pumping lane.** Blocks every ~3h; storage kit; freezer bank covers Monday chaos.

→ It doesn't fit your job/body reality:
→ **Combo lane.** Breast at home mornings/nights + formula at childcare. Zero shame - fed, loved, employed. Check prep rules with your caregiver (they prepare per formula tin instructions exactly; never dilute).

→ Circumstances say exclusive formula: **Formula lane.** Buy the good cooler anyway; your hands stay free of pump parts and your lunch break is actually yours. Same love, different container.

## Any-lane logistics check

- Where will milk/formula live at childcare? Labelled? Cold chain?
- Who thaws/preps? Written instructions given?
- Backup feed in their cupboard Day 1?

## If the employer pushback happens

Re-read your rights block in the Guide. Respond in writing: "Happy to solve logistics together - my request matches [PUMP Act 2022 / HSE guidance / company policy]." Escalation path: HR written → ACAS/Maternity Action (UK) → DOL complaint (US) → union/labour officer (NG).

## Mood guardrail

If feeding-at-work becomes daily dread or grief spirals: that's data, not weakness. Lactation consultant (lane change is allowed!) + the Rescue Card numbers. Fed-and-well beats any badge.

# Scripts for the Working Mother
*Employer. Caregiver. Partner. Yourself.*

## 1 · The employer ask (email, send D-8)

> "As I plan my return on [date], I'd like to confirm two adjustments: (1) a private, non-bathroom space and reasonable break windows to express milk (as covered by the PUMP Act / supported under HSE guidance / our policy), and (2) [flex start / WFH Wed]. Happy to propose a schedule that keeps coverage seamless. Thank you!"

## 2 · Pushback on pump breaks

> "I've checked this against [the law/guidance] and I'm confident we can make it work logistically. Here's the coverage plan so nothing slips: [blocks + handover note]. Let's agree the space this week."

## 3 · Childcare briefing card (hand over printed)

> "Feeds: ___ml every ___h (or on demand). Storage: fridge only, dated labels. Soothing: she likes the shoulder bounce + shhh. Emergency contact order: [x, y, z]. Photo updates melt me. THANK YOU."

## 4 · Partner renegotiation (the invisible-load talk)

> "Work added a job to both of us. Tonight we split the NEW jobs list, not argue about the old one: nights alternate, mornings you own [x], I own [y], Sunday we trade sleep-ins. We're teammates, not scorekeepers."

## 5 · The 5pm guilt spiral (say to yourself)

> "She had food, warmth, and a person who loves her all day. So did I. We reunite now and the evening is ours."

## 6 · Declining the 'just one more thing' at work while stabilizing

> "I want to do this properly rather than fast - give me until Thursday to scope it. Right now I have a hard stop at 5."

## 7 · When it gets dark (to a real human)

> "I'm not adjusting okay. This is heavier than tired. Can you [come over / take Thursday]?" + book the clinician. Numbers on the rescue card. Asking is the strong version of you.
