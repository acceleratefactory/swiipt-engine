The deeper segmentation I would use

For your Transformation IP research, I wouldn't organize the opportunity library simply as:

Postpartum → C-section → breastfeeding → sleep

I'd build it around situations.

LEVEL 1 — Life State

Pregnant → Baby arrives → First 2 weeks → First 6 weeks → 3 months → 6 months → Return to work → First year

LEVEL 2 — Situation

For example:

Baby arrives → mother recovering from C-section → partner works during day → no nearby family → newborn wakes frequently

LEVEL 3 — Specific problem

"I am exhausted, recovering from surgery, breastfeeding, and still expected to cook and manage the house."

LEVEL 4 — Desired transformation

"Get the household functioning for the next 14 days without the mother carrying the workload."

That is where I think your gold is.
