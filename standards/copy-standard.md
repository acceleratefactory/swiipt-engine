# COPY STANDARD
*Factory standards file · derived from pipeline.md §12 (copy generated from the record), MEMORY.md locked title architecture + naming principle, Standard v1 §15 Tests K/Q/N, evidence-label rules.*

## 1 · Source of truth

Copy is **generated from the Product Record + Transformation Record + this standard + the
customer situation + evidence** — never free-written from a prompt. The landing page cannot
accidentally drift from the actual transformation. Outputs: `landing-page.json`,
`product-page.json`, `faq.json`, `specs.json`, `deliverables.json`, `seo.json`.

## 2 · Title architecture (LOCKED 2026-08-19)

Every product has three layers:

- **TITLE** — emotionally/situationally recognizable: the buyer's language, a specific moment,
  immediate "this is for me" recognition. Carries the situation, NOT the explanation.
- **SUBTITLE** — explains the transformation/system.
- **ONE-LINE PROMISE** — states the outcome we can responsibly promise.

Naming principle: **`Situation + catchy phrase` ✅ / `Topic + catchy phrase` ❌.**
Name the buyer's specific problem in language she recognizes; leave the mechanism to the product.
Test Q: does the title communicate the recognizable situation without pretending to explain the
mechanism?

## 3 · Voice

- The customer's own words for her pain (drawn from research evidence quotes), not clinical labels.
- **Primary voice is globally/Western relatable.** Cultural specificity (Nigerian/African/diaspora
  contexts — omugwo, aso-ebi, village dynamics, extended-family expectations) appears where it
  names a real situation more precisely, as a deliberate second entry door or wedge — it never
  becomes the product's default identity. A Western reader must never feel the product wasn't
  written for her; an African reader must feel deeply seen. Both from the same record.
- No marketing prose where plain instruction serves better — copy is load-bearing only.

## 4 · Honesty rules

- Every claim carries its evidence label (`sourced_evidence / expert_reviewed /
  lived_experience / model_inference / hypothesis`) wherever used publicly.
- No fabricated testimonials; composites must be labeled.
- No promised outcomes beyond the TSM and evidence basis; remaining limits stay visible.
- Educational trust line on commercial surfaces:
  "Educational content — not medical, clinical, or mental-health advice."
- SEO structured data is truthful-only (no invented ratings/reviews).

## 5 · Safety-adjacent copy

- Never write "call your local crisis line" — give the actual verified number.
- No diagnosis language; products are screens + routers, not therapy.
- No "cure/heal in N days" framing.
