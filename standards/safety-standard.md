# SAFETY STANDARD
*Factory standards file · derived from Standard v1 section 21 (human authority) and Gates 4-5; MEMORY.md safety rules + verified crisis/escalation list; publication gate framework.*

## 1 - Prime rule

Products are screens and routers - **never diagnosis, never therapy**. The AI Navigator and all
products help identify/navigate situations; they never diagnose mental illness or provide
treatment. No product operates outside its competence boundary (AI Test L).

## 2 - Evidence rules (Gate 4)

- Claims carry status labels: sourced_evidence / expert_reviewed / lived_experience /
  model_inference / hypothesis. Clinical facts and thresholds CANNOT be invented.
- Red-flag thresholds (bleeding timelines, fever limits, mood red flags, pelvic-floor warning
  signs) must come from authoritative literature - never from memory. Products translate sourced
  evidence into escalation language ("see a pelvic-health physiotherapist if X").
- Unverified statistics are re-verified or cut. Marketing claims attributed to their source.
- Gate sequence: evidence label -> source-backed safety boundaries -> product content -> QA -> publish.

## 3 - Escalation routing

Three-tier routing ends every self-check in every clinical-adjacent product:
RED = EMERGENCY NOW · AMBER = URGENT TODAY · GREEN = SUPPORTED THIS WEEK.

- Use ONLY the verified shared crisis/escalation list (Nigeria: 112/199 emergency, SURPIN, MANI,
  DV lines; UK: 999 / Samaritans 116 123; US: 911 / 988 / text HOME to 741741; Canada: 911 / 988).
  Re-verify the list every 6 months.
- Never write "call your local crisis line" - give the actual number.
- Never imply Nigeria has a 988-style number.
- PSI is not a crisis line - GREEN/AMBER situations only. Never let a screen output a self-diagnosis.

## 4 - Content safety rules

- No "cure/heal in N days" framing on recovery products.
- Formula: never diluted, never endorsed for dilution. Alcohol-based galactagogues: explicit warning always.
- Safe sleep: African co-sleeping handled with risk-minimisation, not Western "never co-sleep" absolutism.
- Mental-health products: screens + routers only; escalation/red-flag cards required for all
  clinical products; no fabricated testimonials (composites must be labeled).
- Sensitive products use the SENSITIVE experience mode; no decorative treatment that trivializes
  the situation.

## 5 - Human authority (Standard v1 section 21 - non-negotiable)

Humans retain authority over: transformation boundary · high-risk medical/safety approval ·
major claims · pricing strategy · brand-level exceptions · publication override.
**The AI cannot override a safety gate. The builder cannot approve its own work. The publisher
cannot repair an incomplete product. The research agent cannot decide a finding is worthless.**

## 6 - Publication gate framework (owner-defined)

> Specified -> Drafted -> Clinically/safety reviewed where necessary -> Internally
> consistency-checked -> Rendered in final delivery format -> Tested as a complete customer
> journey -> Publication-ready

Clinical-adjacent products publish reviewer credentials + disclaimer. Only the owner clears
Gate N (evidence labels) and Gate O (clinical review). Production rule (locked): the owner decides
the promise and product boundary; evidence decides clinical facts and safety thresholds; the
factory translates both into the customer transformation experience.

## 7 - Context-aware escalation (safety held, expression local)

Safety thresholds and escalation paths **never weaken by customer context** — the same crisis
routes, red-flag thresholds, and clinical boundaries apply globally. Expression may localize
(contact numbers, available services, language) via Context Adapters, but the escalation
instruction itself does not dilute. A customer context that lacks a listed service resolves to the
nearest equivalent or the universal route — never to silence.
