## Post copy (verbatim from asset record)

**Hook:** A request creates a favour. A schedule creates a default. Giving your partner a named shift is a status upgrade, not charity.

**Problem:** Asking directly for help can feel like an accusation. The partner's one-off gesture resets the expectation: he helped once, so the default remains she owns all of it.

**Bold line:** Reframes the partner from 'helper' to 'co-manager' — a status upgrade instead of a request for charity. A written roster removes the emotional labour of asking: the schedule asks, not her.
