## Post copy (verbatim from asset record)

**Hook:** A roster does not require a partner. It requires a named shift and a named person to cover it — a support web, not a marriage contract. The night still needs an owner and a schedule.

**Problem:** Nobody owns the night, so the default becomes she owns all of it. Doing 100% of nights, every night, for weeks — with no partner shift to hand off to.

**Bold line:** Schedules a support-web roster — named shifts for family, friends, or paid help — so the mother's protected block is physically real. A sleep banker makes exhaustion visible and tradeable even without a partner.
