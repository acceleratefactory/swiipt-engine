## Visual copy (on image, verbatim from paired design spec)

> One parent wakes. The other sleeps through it. By morning, neither feels seen. The resentment spirals.

> A roster does not fix the exhaustion. It fixes the resentment. That is the real difference.

## Post copy (verbatim from asset record)

**Carousel post copy (verbatim):**

1. (hook) A request creates a favour. A schedule creates a default. The night does not need more goodwill. It needs an owner and a roster.
2. (problem) Every uninterrupted hour he gets feels like proof her exhaustion is invisible, and naming it makes her the difficult one.
3. (mechanism) A written roster replaces silent bookkeeping with an observable system. The sleep banker makes exhaustion visible and tradeable — credits and paybacks turn private resentment into a ledger both adults can see.
4. (desired) Nights with zero protected block become a protected block of 3+ hours on most nights. 'Whose turn' arguments drop from several times a week to once a week or less. The partner initiates his shift without being reminded.
5. (cta) The score is kept in your head. Every uninterrupted hour he gets feels like proof your exhaustion is invisible. Naming it makes you the difficult one.
