## Post copy (verbatim from asset record)

**Carousel post copy (verbatim):**

1. (hook) A roster does not require a partner. It requires a named shift and a named person to cover it — a support web, not a marriage contract. The night still needs an owner and a schedule.
2. (problem) Nobody owns the night, so the default becomes she owns all of it. Doing 100% of nights, every night, for weeks — with no partner shift to hand off to.
3. (mechanism) Schedules a support-web roster — named shifts for family, friends, or paid help — so the mother's protected block is physically real. A sleep banker makes exhaustion visible and tradeable even without a partner.
4. (desired) Nights with zero protected block become a protected block of 3+ hours on most nights. The support web covers named shifts instead of the mother owning every night alone.
5. (cta) Nobody helps you; you are all by yourself. Cultural norms assign night care to the mother by default, and there is no agreed system. A roster does not require a partner — it requires a named shift and a named person to cover it. Build a support web, not a marriage contract. Give the night a schedule tonight.
