## Visual copy (on image, verbatim from paired design spec)

> The real cost of the night argument is not the lost sleep. It is the resentment that stays awake long after the baby sleeps.

> See the system that ends the argument

## Post copy (verbatim from asset record)

**Hook:** A request creates a favour. A schedule creates a default. The night does not need more goodwill. It needs an owner and a roster.

**Problem:** Every uninterrupted hour he gets feels like proof her exhaustion is invisible, and naming it makes her the difficult one.

**Bold line:** A written roster replaces silent bookkeeping with an observable system. The sleep banker makes exhaustion visible and tradeable — credits and paybacks turn private resentment into a ledger both adults can see.
