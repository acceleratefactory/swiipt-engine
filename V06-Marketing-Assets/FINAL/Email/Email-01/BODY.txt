It is 3:14 in the morning and before you stand up the question is already running: is it my turn, or is he getting up?

You do not know, because nobody wrote it down. So you get up. You have got up every night this week.

Here is the part almost nobody says out loud: this is usually not a story about a bad partner. It is a story about a missing system. When there is no agreed default, every night has to be decided again by two people who have not slept - and the person who is already awake keeps deciding it. The default quietly becomes: you own all of it.

That is why "can you help more?" produces one Saturday morning and then resets. A request creates a favour. A schedule creates a default.
