APPROVED CAPTIONS (verbatim)

Opening Hook:
A request creates a favour. A schedule creates a default. The night does not need more goodwill. It needs an owner and a roster.

Problem Narrative:
Every uninterrupted hour he gets feels like proof her exhaustion is invisible, and naming it makes her the difficult one. 'Can you help more?' produced a one-off gesture, not a system.

Desired Change:
Nights with zero protected block become a protected block of 3+ hours on most nights. 'Whose turn' arguments drop from several times a week to once a week or less. The partner initiates his shift without being reminded.

Safety Line:
This is general parenting information — not medical advice.
