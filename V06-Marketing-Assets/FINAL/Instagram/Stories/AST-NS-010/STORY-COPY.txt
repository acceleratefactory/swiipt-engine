AST-NS-010
========================================================
1 (hook):
A request creates a favour. A schedule creates a default. Giving your partner a named shift is a status upgrade, not charity.

2 (problem):
Asking directly for help can feel like an accusation. The partner's one-off gesture resets the expectation: he helped once, so the default remains she owns all of it.

3 (insight):
Reframes the partner from 'helper' to 'co-manager' — a status upgrade instead of a request for charity. A written roster removes the emotional labour of asking: the schedule asks, not her.

4 (cta):
Nights with zero protected block become a protected block of 3+ hours on most nights. 'Whose turn' arguments drop from several times a week to once a week or less. The partner initiates his shift without being reminded.
