NARRATION (verbatim)

There is a difference between being available and being on duty - and it is the whole reason night plans fail. Available means: I am in the house, wake me if it gets bad. On duty means: this block is mine, the alarm is set, I am awake and I am handling it. Available still leaves the decision with the exhausted person. On duty removes it entirely. Write down who is on duty, not who is around.
