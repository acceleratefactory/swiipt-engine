TITLE: How a fridge chart stops the 3 AM negotiation
SITUATION: the fridge chart
FORMAT: YouTube Short (vertical 9:16), 20-60s

============================================================
NARRATION
============================================================

This is the least glamorous thing in the whole system and it does the most work: a fridge chart. Two clock times, one name per block, written by hand, in the same place every day. It goes on the fridge and not in an app because information you have to open is information you will renegotiate. Tonight is already decided. So the only question left at 3 AM is a practical one, not a fairness one.
