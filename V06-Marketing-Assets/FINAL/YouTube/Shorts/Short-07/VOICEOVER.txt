NARRATION (verbatim)

If you are the off-duty parent, your job is not nothing - it is specific. Two things. First, protect your own sleep properly: earplugs, white noise, because that is the point of a shift system and being tired tomorrow helps nobody. Second, own your whole block when it comes: the resettles, the nappy, the pacing, the soothing. Your partner should be able to hear the baby and do nothing. That is what a real block means.
