One parent is running every night alone - feeding, settling, soothing - while the other sleeps, works away, or believes 'helping' means one nappy change. There is no agreed system, so every night is renegotiated by exhaustion and resentment.

This video explains the operating system used instead: an owner, a roster, a scheduled hand-off, a sleep banker, and a rule that the baby always overrides the plan.
