========================================================
Hook:
The breastfeeding objection is real — and solvable. The contract does not pretend otherwise. It schedules a hand-off so that your protected block is real, not fictional.

Problem:
Often still breastfeeding, so 'sleep through the night' is not simply available to her. The partner's objection ('you're breastfeeding anyway') feels unanswerable.

Bold line:
Schedules milk hand-off (a pumped bottle, or your partner brings the baby to you to feed and returns her to the crib) so the mother's protected block is physically real — not a sleep-through-the-night promise.
