TITLE: decide at 8pm, not 2am
FORMAT: TikTok (vertical 9:16), 20-60s

NARRATION
============================================================

Almost every night argument is a timing problem. You are trying to make a decision at the worst possible moment, with the least possible judgement, about the most emotionally loaded thing in the house. Move the decision to 8 PM. Five minutes. Who is on tonight, do the times still make sense, is anything unusual tomorrow? Adjust tonight only. Then the night contains no negotiation at all - only the plan you already agreed.
