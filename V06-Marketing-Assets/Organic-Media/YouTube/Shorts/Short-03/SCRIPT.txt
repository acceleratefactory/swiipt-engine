TITLE: Decide who is on duty before bedtime
SITUATION: the 8 PM decision
FORMAT: YouTube Short (vertical 9:16), 20-60s

============================================================
NARRATION
============================================================

The 3 AM argument is really an 8 PM gap. By 3 AM you are not deciding a roster - you are deciding who is more tired, which no one can do well. So make the decision before the night starts. Two clock times, one name per block, written down. Tonight is already decided. That is the whole trick: you do not need more patience at 3 AM, you need the decision to have been made at 8 PM.
