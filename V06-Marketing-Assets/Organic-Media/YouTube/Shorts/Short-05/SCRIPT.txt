TITLE: One parent works tomorrow - what actually changes
SITUATION: the work-night constraint
FORMAT: YouTube Short (vertical 9:16), 20-60s

============================================================
NARRATION
============================================================

One parent works tomorrow. That is real - and it is not a reason for the other parent to own every single night. A working parent usually cannot own 1 AM to 5 AM. They can often own 9 PM to midnight, before their own sleep, or 5 AM to 7 AM, after it. The work night changes the size of the block, not whether one exists. Write the block you can actually keep.

CTA
============================================================

Work-night blocks and paybacks are already in the shift chart.
