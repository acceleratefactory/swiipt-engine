NARRATION (verbatim)

This is what a newborn night roster actually looks like. It is not a parenting philosophy. It is a page. One block for the working parent before their sleep. One block around the feeds, with the hand-off written in: your partner brings the baby to you, you feed, your partner takes the baby back. One protected block that is genuinely yours. And a rule at the bottom: the baby always overrides the roster. That is the whole system, on one page, decided before the night.
