You do not start with a whole system. You start with one page.

Tonight's Shift Chart is two clock times and one name per block. That is it. It goes on the fridge, handwritten, in the same place every day - because information you have to open is information you will renegotiate.

There are three templates, and the routing question is one line: who is available at night?

Template A - a partner present and willing, breastfeeding-compatible: named blocks, with the feed hand-off written in.
Template B - formula or expressed feeding: two long windows, alternating nights.
Roster C - a partner works away, or there is no partner in the home: a support web, protecting one morning block through a relative, a friend, or paid help.

Before night one, both adults agree four rules: infant safe sleep is paramount; whoever is on shift stays fully awake; the parent on shift uses no earplugs or sleep aids (the off-duty parent may); and the baby always overrides the roster - the agreed phrase is "I need a handoff."

Fifteen minutes tonight. Write the times. Send one line from the script pack. That is the first win.
