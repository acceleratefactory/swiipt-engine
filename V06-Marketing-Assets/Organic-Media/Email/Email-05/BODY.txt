Everything in this series is one piece of a single system - Every Night, Just Me.

What is inside:

- The Shift Chart with all three templates (A breastfeeding-compatible, B alternating, C support web) and the safety guardrails both adults agree before night one
- The Decision Tree - route to your model in one question
- The Fridge Shift Chart - the wall plan that removes the 3 AM negotiation
- The 14-Day Tracker - facts only, with the day-7 and day-30 reviews
- Seven Script Cards - the exact words for the seven hardest moments, including "I have work tomorrow", "I don't hear the baby" and "my mother says this is your job"
- The Bad-Night Rescue Card - the fallback protocol, the no-blame resets, and the escalation boundaries

It is educational coordination support for how two adults divide the night. It is not baby sleep training, and it is not a substitute for paediatric or mental-health care.

If the nights are still being decided at 3 AM, this is the version where the decisions are already made.
