## Post copy (verbatim from asset record)

**Hook:** A request creates a favour. A schedule creates a default. The night does not need more goodwill. It needs an owner and a roster.

**Problem:** Nobody owns the night, so the default becomes you own all of it. Doing 100% of nights, every night, for weeks.

**Bold line:** A written roster removes the emotional labour of asking — the schedule asks, not you. Gives your partner a named shift, not 'help' — a status upgrade, not charity.
