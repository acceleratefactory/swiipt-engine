THE BIGGEST HIDDEN CLUSTER I SEE

After breaking this down, I don't think the deepest market is actually:

Diaspora Money

or

Diaspora Property

or even

Fraud Protection.

I think there is a much more interesting umbrella:

REMOTE OWNERSHIP & FAMILY TRUST OPERATIONS

The person abroad owns or funds something in Nigeria, but cannot physically supervise it.

So they must operate through:

family → representative → agent → contractor → artisan → tenant → supplier.

And the fundamental problem becomes:

How do I maintain visibility and control without personally being there?

That generates an entire family of transformations:

verify before paying
delegate without losing control
document without creating hostility
monitor without physically visiting
separate trust from blind trust
detect problems early
maintain property remotely
manage family money
preserve evidence
maintain continuity if something happens to me.

That is much more interesting than a generic “diaspora finance” niche.

DIASPORA FAMILY FINANCIAL BOUNDARIES

Those are five different markets, not one.
