The real opportunity is underneath them: the recurring situations where living with a chronic condition collides with ordinary life.

And the biggest principle for this Life Area

Be especially careful here because "condition-specific" is not the same thing as "situation-specific."

For example:

❌ IBS Management
❌ Diabetes Meal Planning
❌ GERD Diet Guide
❌ ADHD Eating System
❌ Chronic Disease Symptom Tracker

These are still topics.

But:

"I have IBS and I'm terrified of being trapped in a two-hour meeting without a bathroom."

"I have diabetes but I'm the person who cooks dinner for my entire family every night."

"I have ADHD and every meal plan collapses because deciding, shopping, cooking and cleaning require too many executive-function steps."

"I have GERD and my nights are being destroyed because I can't predict which dinner will trigger reflux."

"I've been diagnosed with a chronic condition and my doctor will ask me what has happened over the last three months—but my health information is scattered everywhere."

Those are the markets.

And from those situations, we can later derive the Transformation → ATUs → Knowledge Blocks → Asset/Tool → Product architecture without losing the underlying opportunity.
