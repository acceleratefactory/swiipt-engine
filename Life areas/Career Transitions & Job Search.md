THE MOST IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

If we are doing this to eventually build a transformation library, I would not let the research team return with products like:

Resume Template
Interview Guide
Career Change Ebook
Salary Negotiation Checklist

Those are assets, not necessarily markets.

The market unit should look like:

Mid-career professional → wants to leave a successful but unsustainable career → doesn't want to lose 15 years of accumulated expertise → can't afford entry-level reset → doesn't know which adjacent careers will value existing capital → feels trapped between financial security and personal dissatisfaction → wants a credible transition into a better-fit career without starting over.

That can produce multiple transformation products:

career-direction diagnostic
transferable-capital mapper
adjacent-career decision system
transition roadmap
experience translation tool
job-search positioning system
portfolio/evidence builder
offer comparison tool
transition tracker
decision simulator

The market comes first; the asset comes later.

And just like Postpartum, I would keep pushing until we can describe the customer with enough precision that you can almost see the person sitting at their laptop at 11:30 p.m., trying to solve the problem.

That is when we know we've moved from “career market” to a genuine hidden submarket.
