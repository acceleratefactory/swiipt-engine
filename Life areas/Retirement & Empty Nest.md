01 — “I DON'T KNOW WHO I AM WITHOUT MY JOB”
Broad topic

Retirement & Empty Nest

→ Submarket

Retirement identity

→ Specific life situation

A person approaching retirement after decades in one career.

→ Specific person

Career-focused professional whose identity is heavily tied to work.

→ Trigger

Retirement date becomes real.

→ Failed attempt / constraint

They tell themselves:

“I'll just relax and figure it out.”

But have no idea what daily life should look like.

→ Emotional stake

“What if the most important part of my life is already over?”

→ Desired transformation

Career identity → meaningful post-career identity.

This is much stronger than “retirement planning.”

02 — THE PERSON WHO DOESN'T WANT TO RETIRE

Retirement

→ Submarket

→ Constraint

→ Emotional stake

→ Transformation

03 — RETIREMENT COUNTDOWN

excitement + fear.

Transformation:
