# 5\. Product Discovery Rule

Do not turn this into generic disease/topic ebooks. Use: Topic/life stage → recurring situation → transformation → mechanism/system → product.

Example: “Menopause Management” is broad. A specific situation such as repeated 2AM waking, work brain fog, unpredictable periods, or newly diagnosed PCOS is a transformation territory.

# 6\. Claude Code Implementation Rules

-   Create Women's Hormonal & Reproductive Health as a top-level Life Area.
-   Treat the mapped territories as Libraries/Categories and candidate situation territories, not automatic commercial products.
-   Preserve buyer-language situation names because they support search, SEO, Situation Finder routing and the Transformation Engine.
-   Represent cross-library links, especially Postpartum ↔ Reproductive Health, PCOS ↔ Fertility, and symptom/disease ↔ appointment navigation.
-   Hidden markets are discovery targets, not automatic products.
-   Generate products from validated situations and transformations, not from category names alone.
-   Medical topics require appropriate evidence labels, safety boundaries and professional-escalation pathways.
-   Use the taxonomy as a Transformation Engine journey graph, not merely a flat WooCommerce category tree.
