THE REAL UNIT WE SHOULD EVENTUALLY RESEARCH

For example, don't research:

“Delegation.”

Research:

First-time manager → promoted from top individual contributor → still doing their old work because they don't trust the team → team keeps bringing decisions back to them → manager works nights to keep up → feels like promotion has made life worse → wants to build a team that can make decisions without them.

That is a market.

Or:

New external manager → inherits a team with low trust after a bad predecessor → team resists every change → manager can't tell legitimate resistance from dysfunctional behavior → makes one premature change and loses more trust → fears being labelled a bad leader → wants to diagnose and reset the team without creating another management crisis.

That is a market.

Or:

High-performing manager → promoted again → now manages managers → discovers that solving individual problems personally no longer scales → spends entire week in meetings and escalations → feels less effective despite having a bigger title → wants to transition from operational manager to strategic leader.

That is a market.

And that is the level I would keep pushing this Life Area toward.

The goal isn't to produce 100 management topics. The goal is to uncover 30–60 extremely specific human situations where the person is experiencing a transition, failure, constraint, fear, or identity disruption and has a strong reason to want a transformation.

That is the same discipline that made the Postpartum breakdown useful—and it is the discipline I would preserve across the remaining Life Areas.
