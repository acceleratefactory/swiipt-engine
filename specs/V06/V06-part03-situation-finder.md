Before choosing a schedule, we must diagnose your current night reality. Answer the three questions below honestly. Your answers route you to the exact roster and scripts built for your situation.

---

## Question 1: What is your primary feeding method?

- **A.** I am exclusively breastfeeding (direct latch only, no bottles).
- **B.** I am breastfeeding but can pump expressed milk, or we combo-feed with formula.
- **C.** We feed exclusively with formula or expressed milk.

---

## Question 2: What is your partner's availability at night?

- **A.** My partner is home at night but is minimally involved or sleeps through.
- **B.** My partner works night shifts, travels constantly, or lives in another city.
- **C.** I am parenting solo — I do not have a partner in the home.

---

## Question 3: What is your primary source of household friction right now?

- **A.** My partner says they "want to help" but sleeps through the cries or doesn't know what to do.
- **B.** We constantly argue at night about whose turn it is or who is more tired.
- **C.** Extended family members (mother, mother-in-law, aunties) say night duty is solely the woman's job and criticize my partner's involvement.
- **D.** I am too exhausted to coordinate anything — we are in absolute survival mode.
