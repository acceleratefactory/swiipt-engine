# V06 — Part 11: The Rescue Card (Bad Night Protocol)
## Every Night, Just Me
### A postpartum night-coordination system that replaces nightly arguing with a pre-agreed roster, a fairness ledger, and the exact words to make it stick.

> **Product:** V06 | **Module:** 11 of 13 | **Type:** Emergency Protocol / Rescue Card
> **Evidence label:** "Educational content informed by relationship research, postpartum health guidance, and lived experience. It is not a substitute for medical, clinical, or mental-health assessment."

---

*Use this protocol when the baby is sick, teething, going through a growth spurt, or when the roster has collapsed completely. This is not a failure — it is a planned recovery route.*

---

## Step 1: Stop and Assess the Baby First

Before anything else — check the baby.

If the baby has a fever, is vomiting, is showing signs of unusual pain, has difficulty breathing, or you have any concern about their physical wellbeing — **suspend the roster immediately** and follow your pediatric guidance or contact emergency services.

- 🇳🇬 Nigeria: **112**
- 🇬🇧 United Kingdom: **999**
- 🇺🇸🇨🇦 USA / Canada: **911**

The roster resumes when the baby is stable and you have appropriate support in place. The roster never takes priority over the baby's health.

---

## Step 2: The 3-Step Survival Routine

If the baby is assessed and safe but the night is simply impossible — teething, growth spurt, inconsolable crying — do this:

**1. Default to the On-Duty Manager**
The parent whose shift it currently is handles the initial response. Do not wake the off-duty parent unless you have exhausted your options and reached your limit.

**2. Execute a Tag-Team Rotation**
If the baby will not settle at all and the situation is not improving: switch to an **hourly tag-team** — Parent A takes the first hour, Parent B takes the second hour. One parent rests while the other is on. Do not both stay awake — maintaining one person's functional capacity is the goal.

**3. Trigger the Automatic Recovery Credit**
Any night where the on-duty parent activates the emergency handoff and the off-duty parent takes over automatically earns the covering parent **+1 Night Shift Credit** in the Recovery Bank. Write it on the ledger in the morning. No discussion needed.

---

## Step 3: The Morning After

Do not review, debrief, or assign blame the same morning. Both adults are likely running on near-zero. Agree on one thing only:

> *"We got through last night. We will do the 5-minute reset tonight before the shift starts."*

Then rest. Debrief at the Sunday Roster Review, not at 7 AM on a broken night.
