## ROSTER TEMPLATE A: The Breastfeeding-Compatible Split Shift

*For couples where the baby is exclusively breastfed. The partner manages all non-feeding operations during his shift, protecting the mother's sleep opportunity blocks.*

---

### POSTPARTUM SHIFT AGREEMENT — ROSTER A

**1. THE GOAL**
To protect a minimum of four (4) hours of uninterrupted sleep opportunity for **[Mother's Name]** every night to prevent cognitive breakdown, while ensuring **[Baby's Name]** is safely cared for.

**2. THE SHIFT ROSTER**

**First Block — Partner's Shift**
- Hours: **9:00 PM – 2:00 AM** (adjust to your household — write your actual times below)
- My hours: ________ PM to ________ AM
- **Active Shift Manager:** [Partner's Name] — must remain alert and awake while caring for the baby.
- **Duties:** Diapering, burping, soothing, and resettling the baby in the crib.
- **Feeding Protocol:** Baby is fed by the mother immediately before the shift starts. If the baby wakes during this shift:
  - Partner diaper-checks and soothes the baby first.
  - If the baby needs a feed, partner brings the baby to the mother in a dark room. Mother feeds without turning on bright lights. Immediately after feeding, partner takes the baby back — diaper, burp, soothe, resettle. Mother returns to sleep immediately.

**Second Block — Mother's Shift**
- Hours: **2:00 AM – 7:00 AM** (adjust to match your first block end time)
- My hours: ________ AM to ________ AM
- **Active Shift Manager:** [Mother's Name]
- **Duties:** All feeding, diapering, and soothing duties. Partner is off-duty and sleeps uninterrupted.

**3. BOUNDARY RULES**
- **The Off-Duty Rule:** During the partner's shift, the mother is officially off-duty. She sleeps in a separate room or uses earplugs/white noise to protect her sleep opportunity. She does not get up to investigate unless called upon by the partner.
- **The Escalation Clause:** If the baby cannot be settled, or the caregiver is concerned about feeding, illness, injury, breathing, or any unusual behavior — the active caregiver assesses the baby and follows their existing pediatric or clinical guidance, or seeks appropriate help. They may coordinate a support handoff using the agreed phrase: *"I need a handoff."*

## ROSTER TEMPLATE B: The Alternating / Bottle-Fed Shift

*For couples using formula or expressed breastmilk. Allows a true alternating shift — both parents receive a solid 4–5 hour sleep opportunity window.*

---

### POSTPARTUM SHIFT AGREEMENT — ROSTER B

**1. THE GOAL**
To split night duties equally, ensuring both parents receive a minimum of five (5) hours of uninterrupted sleep opportunity.

**2. THE SHIFT ROSTER**

**First Shift — Partner's Shift**
- Hours: **10:00 PM – 3:00 AM** (adjust to your household — write your actual times below)
- My hours: ________ PM to ________ AM
- **Active Shift Manager:** [Partner's Name]
- **Duties:** Bottle-feed (formula or expressed milk), burp, diaper change, soothe, and resettle the baby. Mother is off-duty and sleeps in a separate sleep zone.

**Second Shift — Mother's Shift**
- Hours: **3:00 AM – 8:00 AM** (adjust to match your first shift end time)
- My hours: ________ AM to ________ AM
- **Active Shift Manager:** [Mother's Name]
- **Duties:** Breastfeed or bottle-feed, burp, diaper change, soothe, and resettle. Partner is off-duty and sleeps uninterrupted.

**3. BOUNDARY RULES**
- **Handoff Alarm:** A physical alarm is set for the transition time. The handoff must be silent and swift. The oncoming manager takes over immediately; the outgoing manager goes to sleep.
- **Prep Rule:** Before the first shift starts, all bottles, formula, or expressed breastmilk must be pre-measured and placed at the feeding station. No night-time hunting for supplies.

## ROSTER TEMPLATE C: If You Don't Have a Partner Available (Support-Web Roster)

*For single mothers, mothers whose partners are frequently away, or those using a helper — a family member, night nanny, or trusted friend.*

---

### POSTPARTUM SHIFT AGREEMENT — ROSTER C

**1. THE GOAL**
To protect the mother's physical recovery and mental wellness by scheduling support-web shifts, ensuring she gets a minimum 4-hour sleep opportunity block.

**2. THE SCHEDULED VILLAGE**
- **Night Helper / Family Member / Paid Support:** [Helper's Name]
- **Active Support Nights:** [Days of the week — e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday]
- **Shift Block:** **11:00 PM – 4:00 AM** (write your actual times below)
- My hours: ________ PM to ________ AM
- **Active Shift Manager:** [Helper's Name]
- **Duties:** Diaper, soothe, and bottle-feed (formula or expressed milk). If direct breastfeeding is required, the helper brings the baby to the mother for feeding only, then immediately takes the baby back to burp, diaper, and resettle. Mother's role during this block: sleep.

**3. EMERGENCY BACKUP — NIGHTS WITH NO HELPER**
On nights when no helper is scheduled, the mother invokes the **Survival Protocol:**
- Sleep when the baby sleeps — no exceptions.
- Defer all non-essential household tasks.
- Use pre-cooked or frozen meals.
- Restrict daytime visits to protect rest.
