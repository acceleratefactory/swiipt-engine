# V06 — Part 12: The Re-entry Protocol
## Every Night, Just Me
### A postpartum night-coordination system that replaces nightly arguing with a pre-agreed roster, a fairness ledger, and the exact words to make it stick.

> **Product:** V06 | **Module:** 12 of 13 | **Type:** Re-entry / Relapse Recovery
> **Evidence label:** "Educational content informed by relationship research, postpartum health guidance, and lived experience. It is not a substitute for medical, clinical, or mental-health assessment."

---

## When to Use This

You had a good run — maybe a week, maybe two. Then something happened: a work trip, an illness, a hard season, an argument. The roster quietly stopped. You slid back to the pattern where you do everything alone at night, and now it feels too awkward or too exhausting to restart.

This is a normal relapse point. It is not a sign the system failed. It is a sign the system needs a restart. This protocol is built for exactly this moment.

---

## The 3-Step Re-entry

**Step 1: The 10-Minute Reset Meeting**

Schedule it during the day — not at night, not in the morning after a hard night. Sit down together with the Fridge Shift Chart and the tracker.

No blame. No full debrief. One agenda item only:

> *"We stopped. Let's restart. Which part broke?"*

---

**Step 2: Review the Breakdown Reason**

Look at the tracker or think back honestly. Ask:

- Was the shift transition time unrealistic for your schedule?
- Did a work pattern or travel change make the roster unworkable?
- Did an argument about the roster make it feel easier to abandon it?
- Did the baby's sleep change and the old times no longer fit?

Identify the one real reason. Then adjust that one thing — not the whole system.

Common adjustments:
- Move the transition time by 30–60 minutes to match reality
- Simplify to one non-negotiable block if the full roster feels overwhelming
- Add the Sunday Review back as a hard calendar commitment

---

**Step 3: Adjust, Re-sign, and Restart the Tracker**

Make the adjustment on the roster template. Re-sign the contract — both adults. Restart the 14-Day Shift Tracker from Day 1 with the new times.

You do not restart from the diagnosis phase. You already know your situation and your roster type. You restart from Day 4 (the Run phase) — pick up the system where you know it works and run it again with the adjustment in place.

---

## The One Rule for Re-entry

> No guilt. No lengthy debrief. No "you ruined it." One adjustment. Re-sign. Restart.

The system is designed to be restarted. Use it.
