Welcome to the **Night-Shift Contract System**.

This is not a book to read about sleep deprivation. This is a practical, step-by-step operating intervention for your household. You are about to replace exhaustion-driven nightly arguments with a pre-agreed default system that protects your sleep opportunity.

---

## The 10-Step Journey

1. **Read:** *The Argument That Ends the War* — Understand why unstructured "asking for help" fails.
2. **Find:** *Situation Finder* — Diagnose your household's exact night profile.
3. **Decide:** *The Decision Tree* — Select the roster template engineered for your feeding style and partner availability.
4. **Learn:** *Safety & Boundary Rules* — Establish the non-negotiable safety guardrails for adult vigilance and baby care.
5. **Build:** *The Roster Templates* — Customize your Shift Contract (Roster A, B, or C).
6. **Bank:** *Sleep Banker Mechanics* — Activate the Recovery Bank ledger to trade shifts fairly.
7. **Communicate:** *Script Cards* — Use the precise, battle-tested words to navigate partner objections and community commentary.
8. **Execute:** *The Fridge Shift Chart* — Put the physical chart in a highly visible location (like the refrigerator door).
9. **Track:** *The 14-Day Shift Tracker* — Log your system's performance, friction, and sleep opportunity outcomes daily.
10. **Recover & Restart:** *Rescue Cards & Re-entry Protocols* — Use these specialized protocols when illness, teething, or roster breakdown occurs.
