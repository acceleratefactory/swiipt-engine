## What each route means

**→ Routed to Roster A**
You are breastfeeding and your partner is home. Your partner manages all non-feeding duties during his shift — diapering, soothing, resettling. He brings the baby to you only for feeds, then takes the baby back immediately. You do not get up for anything else.
**Start with:** Part 6 → Roster A Template. Use **Script Cards 1, 2, and 3** to introduce and hold the system.

---

**→ Routed to Roster B**
You can use bottles (formula or expressed milk). This allows a true alternating shift — one parent fully off, one parent fully on. Both get a solid 4–5 hour sleep opportunity window.
**Start with:** Part 6 → Roster B Template. Use **Script Cards 5 and 6** for weekly review and reset.

---

**→ Routed to Roster C**
You are parenting without a partner at night — solo, or partner is away. This roster builds a scheduled support web from your available village: a family member, a paid night helper, or a trusted friend.
**Start with:** Part 6 → Roster C Template. Use **Script Card 7** for managing extended family dynamics.

---

**→ Survival Mode (Q3 Answer D)**
Do not start the roster tonight. Go directly to **Part 11: The Rescue Card (Bad Night Protocol)**. Get through tonight. Return to this Decision Tree tomorrow when you have had any rest at all.
