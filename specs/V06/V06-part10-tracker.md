# V06 — Part 10: The 14-Day Shift Tracker
## Every Night, Just Me
### A postpartum night-coordination system that replaces nightly arguing with a pre-agreed roster, a fairness ledger, and the exact words to make it stick.

> **Product:** V06 | **Module:** 10 of 13 | **Type:** System Performance Log / 14-Day Tracker
> **Evidence label:** "Educational content informed by relationship research, postpartum health guidance, and lived experience. It is not a substitute for medical, clinical, or mental-health assessment."

---

## How to Use This Tracker

Do not just track whether you followed the roster. Track how the **system performs**. This data feeds your Sunday Roster Review and your Day 30 check-in.

Fill in one row every morning — it takes 90 seconds.

---

## Column Key

| Column | What to Record |
|---|---|
| **S.O. Block (hrs)** | Sleep Opportunity Block protected — the hours you were officially off-duty. Target: 4+ hours. |
| **Actual Block (hrs)** | Actual uninterrupted sleep you got within your off-duty block. |
| **Awakenings** | Total number of times the baby woke during the night. |
| **Responder** | Who responded to each awakening — Mother / Partner / Shared. |
| **Handoff** | How the shift transition went — Smooth / Delayed / Missed / Argued. |
| **Partner Initiative** | Did the partner start his shift without being asked? — Initiated / Needed nudge / Slept through. |
| **Exhaustion (1–10)** | Mother's self-rated morning exhaustion level. 1 = rested. 10 = cannot function. |
| **Breakdown / Notes** | Why the roster failed or what caused friction — e.g., Teething, Work alarm, Baby ill, Argued. |

## What to Do With the Data

- **Day 7 review:** Look at the Handoff column. If "Missed" or "Argued" appears more than twice, the transition time or method needs adjusting — not the whole roster.
- **Day 14 review:** Compare Exhaustion scores from Days 1–3 vs Days 12–14. If exhaustion is not trending down, bring the tracker to your Day 14 check-in and review with your support person or healthcare provider.
- **Day 30 check-in:** The Swiipt platform will prompt you. The tracker is your evidence.
