## SECTION 3 — WHAT THIS IS (The Product)

**Section label:** What Every Night, Just Me gives you

**13-module breakdown (render as icon list or card grid):**

1. **Start Here — The 10-Step Roadmap**
   Know exactly where you are going before you begin.

2. **The Argument That Ends the War** *(Short Read)*
   Understand why "can you help more" fails structurally — and what replaces it.

3. **The Situation Finder** *(Self-Triage Quiz)*
   Three questions. Routes you to the exact roster and scripts built for your household.

4. **The Decision Tree**
   Breastfeeding? Bottle? No partner? One diagram routes you to your system.

5. **Roster Safety & Boundary Rules**
   The non-negotiable guardrails both adults agree to before signing the roster.

6. **Three Roster Templates — A, B, and C** *(Fillable Contracts)*
   A: Breastfeeding-compatible split shift.
   B: Alternating full shift for bottle/expressed milk.
   C: Support-web roster for solo mothers or absent partners.

7. **The Sleep Banker / Recovery Bank** *(Fairness Ledger)*
   A credit-and-debit system that makes invisible exhaustion visible — and redeemable — before it turns into resentment.

8. **Seven Script Cards**
   The exact words for: initiating the conversation, defusing "I have work tomorrow," handling the baby-prefers-you excuse, the 2 AM emergency handoff, the Sunday review, the no-blame reset, and extended family criticism.

9. **The Fridge Shift Chart** *(Printable)*
   One page. Both adults see it every night. No app, no memory, no argument.

10. **The 14-Day Shift Tracker**
    Track sleep opportunity blocks, partner initiative, handoff friction, and exhaustion — so you have evidence, not just feelings.

11. **The Rescue Card — Bad Night Protocol**
    What to do when the baby is sick, teething, or the roster collapses. Includes a pocket-sized print version.

12. **The Re-entry Protocol**
    Slid back to the old pattern? Three steps to restart without guilt, blame, or starting over from scratch.

13. **Safety Gate — When Exhaustion Needs More Than a Night Plan**
    A clear checklist that separates ordinary exhaustion from something that needs professional support — with verified crisis numbers for Nigeria, the UK, and North America.

## SECTION 5 — WHO THIS IS FOR

**Section label:** This is for you if

- You are 0–6 months postpartum and doing the majority — or all — of the night care alone.
- You are breastfeeding, bottle-feeding, or combination feeding, and your partner is home but not consistently contributing to nights.
- You have asked for help before and gotten a one-off gesture, not a system.
- You are a solo mother or have an absent partner — and you need to build a support web, not a couple's roster.
- You are done arguing about whose turn it is and ready to replace the argument with a contract.

**Section label:** This is NOT for you if

- You are looking for infant sleep training advice. This product coordinates adults — not babies.
- You need clinical postpartum mental health support. If that is where you are, go directly to Part 13 (Safety Gate) — it routes you to verified crisis support for Nigeria, UK, and North America.

## SECTION 6 — THE PROMISE (What We Can Responsibly Say)

**Section label:** What we promise — and what we don't

We promise you a system. A written roster. A fairness ledger. Precision scripts. A rescue plan. A re-entry route.

We do not promise you a specific number of hours of sleep. We cannot control your baby's needs, your partner's cooperation, or your household's reality. What we can do is remove the structural reasons nights fail — the asking, the negotiating, the invisible debt — and replace them with a default that works even when you are too exhausted to be generous.

**Evidence label (display prominently near the CTA):**
*Educational content informed by relationship research, postpartum health guidance, and lived experience. It is not a substitute for medical, clinical, or mental-health assessment.*
