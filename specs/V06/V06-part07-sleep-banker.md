## What the Recovery Bank Is

The **Recovery Bank** is a fairness accounting system. It translates invisible night exhaustion into visible, tradable credits — so that exhaustion debt does not silently compound into resentment.

Without a ledger, favors disappear. One partner does an extra night and expects recognition. The other forgets it happened. Three weeks later it surfaces as a fight about something unrelated. The Recovery Bank prevents that by making every credit visible and time-bound.

---

## The Rules of Exchange

**The Currency**

| Credit Type | What It Is | How It Is Earned |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Night Shift Credit (NSC)** | Covering a night block of 3–5 hours outside your scheduled roster, allowing the other parent to sleep | The partner who covers the unscheduled block earns +1 NSC |
| **1 Recovery Payback (RP)** | A morning sleep-in (until 11:00 AM) or a weekend afternoon nap of 3 hours, where the other parent takes 100% responsibility for the baby and household | Redeemed by the parent who holds the credit |

**The Ledger Rule**
All credits and debits must be written on the physical Fridge Shift Chart or in the table below. Verbal promises do not count. If it is not written, it did not happen.

**The Expiration Rule**
All Night Shift Credits must be redeemed as Recovery Paybacks within **seven (7) days** of being earned. Any credit not redeemed within 7 days expires automatically. This prevents historical tally-keeping ("You owe me from three weeks ago") and forces timely rest rather than compounding resentment.

## How to Use It — Three Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Partner covers an extra block**
Baby is sick on Wednesday. Partner covers mother's shift (2–7 AM) so she can sleep. Partner earns +1 NSC. Mother writes it on the ledger Wednesday. Partner redeems it as a Saturday morning sleep-in by Sunday at the latest (7-day window).

**Scenario 2: Emergency override**
Any night where the on-duty parent activates the emergency handoff and the off-duty parent takes over automatically earns the covering parent +1 NSC. No discussion needed — the emergency protocol itself triggers the credit.

**Scenario 3: Expired credit**
Partner earned an NSC on Monday. By the following Monday it has not been redeemed. It expires. This is not a punishment — it is a system boundary. The goal is rest now, not debt-tracking later.
