## 01 — PRODUCT IDENTITY
- **Product name:** New-Body Closet (Postpartum Body Recovery & Wardrobe System)
- **Life area:** m01 Postpartum (body-recovery sub-theme)
- **Life state(s):** postpartum-body-recovery, return-to-fit-clothes
- **Trigger(s):** c-section-scar / diastasis / wardrobe-doesn't-fit / body-image
- **Library tier:** Active System (Library Wave 1)
- **Entry doors → one transformation:** body discomfort + "nothing fits" + scar/scar-care anxiety + body-image grief.
- **Target person:** Mother 6 wks–6 mo postpartum, cleared for gentle movement, frustrated that pre-baby clothes don't fit and body feels foreign.
- **Situation:** When a postpartum mother sees her body has changed, she struggles to dress comfortably and recover confidently because her old wardrobe and expectations no longer fit, and wants a system that meets her body where it is.
- **Problem:** Physical discomfort + emotional body-image grief + practical "nothing fits".
- **Constraints:** Limited budget for new clothes, limited time, possible scar/abdominal sensitivity.
- **Desired transformation:** A body-positive, recovery-aware wardrobe + gentle movement system that fits her now.

## 02 — BEFORE STATE
- Experiencing: scar tightness, softness, breast changes, exhaustion.
- Doing now: squeezing into old jeans, avoiding mirrors.
- Not working: "snap back" content that shames.
- Emotional: grief, frustration, occasional shame.
- Practical: wardrobe unwearable; no comfortable go-to outfits.
- Failed attempts: bought shapewear that hurt; followed generic fitness plan too early.
- Environmental: budget tight; laundry volume high.
- People: partner comments (well-meaning or not).

## 03 — AFTER STATE
- Can now: dress comfortably every day without distress.
- Easier: choosing outfits; moving gently without pain.
- Changed: improved body relationship; scar-care confidence.
- Reduced: mirror-avoidance.
- System now: a "new-body closet" capsule + gentle recovery-movement routine.
- Evidence: wears self-chosen outfits; completes scar/movement routine; self-rated body comfort up.

## 04 — TRANSFORMATION MECHANISM
- M1 Accept: reframe "new normal" without shame.
- M2 Audit: build a comfortable capsule from what exists + minimal buys.
- M3 Protect: respond-to-comments script.

## 05 — TRANSFORMATION PATH
Accept → Audit wardrobe → Build capsule → Review fit (2 wks) → Maintain.
