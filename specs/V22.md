## 01 — PRODUCT IDENTITY
- **Product name:** The First Time He Sees My Body Again (Postpartum Body-Image & Intimacy Reconnection)
- **Life area:** m01 Postpartum (body-image / intimacy sub-theme)
- **Life state(s):** postpartum-body-recovery, relationship-reconnection, early-to-mid-postpartum (2–12 months)
- **Trigger(s):** body-exposure-anxiety / stretch-marks-shame / scar-shame / fear-of-intimacy-pain / avoidance-loop
- **Library tier:** Active System (Library Wave 2) — high privacy, sensitive, warm-channel/private-recommendation distribution.
- **Four entry doors → one transformation:**
  - **Door 1 (R07):** "I'm terrified of him seeing or touching my C-section scar/shelf in the light." (Scar intimacy)
  - **Door 2 (R11):** "My loose skin and stretch marks make me feel so foreign that I avoid undressing in front of him." (Body image intimacy)
  - **Door 3 (B7):** "It still hurts or feels tender after the tear/episiotomy, and I'm scared sex will hurt or be ruined forever." (Perineal/pain anxiety)
  - **Door 4 (C3):** "I'm afraid he will stray because I'm not ready for sex, but I can't bring myself to try." (Partner-attraction / straying fear)
- **Target person:** Mother 2–12 months postpartum who is avoiding intimacy or hiding her body from her husband out of physical discomfort, scar fear, or shame about her changed shape.
- **Situation:** When a postpartum mother experiences changes in her skin, scars, or pelvic floor, she struggles to engage in physical intimacy because she fears judgment, rejection, or physical pain, and wants a private, step-by-step emotional and sensory reconnection path.
- **Problem:** Intimacy avoidance, body concealment, partner distance, silent anxiety about partner straying, pain fear.
- **Constraints:** High embarrassment, fear of pushback or misunderstanding from partner, lack of private talk-time due to baby, exhaustion, physical tenderness.
- **Desired transformation:** Confidently returns to physical connection and intimacy on her own terms, without hiding her body or fearing pain, supported by a partner who understands her boundaries.

## 02 — BEFORE STATE
- **Experiencing:** Extreme self-consciousness, hiding behind loose wrappers, getting dressed only in the bathroom, physical tenderness or numbness around C-section scar or perineum, vaginal dryness (breastfeeding-induced hypoestrogenism).
- **Doing now:** Turning off all lights before bed, fabricating fatigue to avoid touch, pulling away when he touches her stomach/hips, checking her body obsessively in the mirror with distress.
- **Not working:** "Pushing through" painful sex (creates secondary vaginismus and trauma); ignoring the distance (leads to resentment and assumptions of rejection by the partner).
- **Emotional:** Shame, grief for her pre-baby body, isolation, panic about partner's fidelity ("straying fear"), feeling like a "milk machine" rather than a wife.
- **Practical:** Every bedroom interaction is a high-stress negotiation or silent retreat; no private time to talk without the baby crying.
- **Failed attempts:** Squeezing into painful shapewear before bed; following generic "spouse pleaser" advice that ignores postpartum physiology; waiting silently for the anxiety to disappear.
- **Environmental:** Nigerian gendered/cultural norms around marital duty vs. postpartum taboos; lack of physical privacy in extended household settings.
- **People involved:** Husband (confused, feeling rejected, or putting subtle pressure); well-meaning relative body comments ("you are still round").

## 03 — AFTER STATE
- **Can now:** Share her physical boundaries clearly, stand in front of her partner without concealing her belly/hips, and experience pain-free, gradual physical connection.
- **Easier:** Talking about intimacy, managing scar/pelvic tenderness, scheduling connection times.
- **Changed:** Restored relationship warmth, acceptance of her postpartum skin/scars, elimination of straying panic.
- **Reduced:** Bedroom avoidance, mirror anxiety, intimacy-related arguments.
- **System now:** A 5-step sensory and physical re-entry ladder + partner script cards + lubrication/comfort guide.
- **Evidence:** Attains comfortable, non-concealed touch; runs the "Intimacy Check-in" ritual; reports self-rated body comfort during touch up; experiences pain-free physical connection.

## 04 — TRANSFORMATION MECHANISM
- **M1 Normalise the Changed Canvas (Body Acceptance):** Dismantle the "snap-back" myth and reframe postpartum marks (stretch marks, C-section scars, soft skin) as structural modifications of motherhood, separating self-worth from pre-pregnancy appearance.
- **M2 Step-by-Step Sensory Re-entry (The Exposure Ladder):** Replace the binary "no sex vs. full sex" pressure with a gradual 5-step touch ladder (clothing-on touch → light-on visibility → scar/area touch without sex → non-coital intimacy → full intimacy).
- **M3 Physiologically Safe Comfort (Pain Defusal):** Address postpartum vaginal dryness and tissue healing with evidence-based mechanics (safe pH-balanced lubricants, scar desensitization massage, pelvic-floor relaxation before contraction).
- **M4 Collaborative Boundaries (Scripts & Roster):** Provide scripts that upgrade the conversation from emotional negotiation ("you don't love me") to structural alignment ("here is my physical map, this is what is safe today").

## 05 — TRANSFORMATION PATH
Reframe Body Image → Mirror Reconciliation → Partner Handoff (The Boundaries Script) → Touch Ladder (Step 1 to 5) → Comfort & Lubrication Protocol → Review & Readjust.

## 06 — ASSET MAP (READ / DO / DECIDE / COMMUNICATE / TRACK / RECOVER)
- **READ:** Swipeable flipbook magazine "The First Time He Sees My Body Again" (beautiful, private, affirming layout addressing body image, scars, and physiological intimacy realities).
- **DO:** The Postpartum Intimacy Map (fillable boundary map where she marks: *Green zones* = feel good; *Amber zones* = gentle/careful; *Red zones* = off-limits due to healing/sensory overwhelm).
- **DECIDE:** "Is it physical pain or emotional anxiety?" triage guide (directing to pelvic-floor physios vs. relationship communication tools).
- **COMMUNICATE:** The Intimacy Script Bank (5 cards):
  - *Card 1:* Explaining the scar/tenderness to him.
  - *Card 2:* Naming the "touched-out" feeling without rejecting him.
  - *Card 3:* Asking for lights-on/no-hiding exposure gradually.
  - *Card 4:* Stopping physical activity mid-way due to pain/discomfort (safe-word protocol).
  - *Card 5:* Defusing comments about body changes.
- **TRACK:** The 5-Step Touch Re-entry Tracker.
- **RECOVER:** The "Intimacy Interrupted" recovery card (what to do when baby wakes up crying mid-moment, or when pain requires an immediate halt).

## 07 — FAILURE-POINT MAP
- **Normal failure:** Husband reacts defensively to boundaries → Use Script Card 2 (reframing as a recovery path, not rejection).
- **Bad day:** Physical pain during contact → Pain Defusal Protocol card, immediately halt, shift to amber-zone massage or holding, apply lubrication.
- **Missed week:** Too tired, zero connection → Re-entry protocol: simplify to 5 minutes of non-demanding holding (Step 1 of Touch Ladder).
- **Resistance (In-law/cultural comments):** Family member comments on her weight/shape → Use Script Card 5 (deflecting comment calmly).
- **Unexpected:** Severe pain or bleeding post-intimacy → Immediate stop, route to pelvic physio/midwife (triage checklist).
- **Overwhelm:** The expectation of "full sex" paralyzes her → Retreat back to Step 2 (holding with clothing on) for a week.
