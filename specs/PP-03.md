## 01 — PRODUCT IDENTITY
- **Product name:** Quiet Leak (Postpartum Bladder / Pelvic-Floor Confidence)
- **Life area:** m01 Postpartum (pelvic-health sub-theme) — [OWNER CONFIRM: may also touch m08/continence-adjacent area]
- **Life state(s):** postpartum-pelvic-recovery, returning-to-activity
- **Trigger(s):** laugh-leak / sneeze-leak / run-leak / fear-of-leak
- **Library tier:** Active System (Library Wave 1) — sensitive, warm-channel distribution (not hard-sell).
- **Four entry doors → one transformation:**
  - **B1-A** — "I leak when I laugh/sneeze" (stress incontinence)
  - **B1-B** — "I'm scared to exercise in case I leak" (avoidance)
  - **B1-C** — "I leak during prayer and I don't know if I'm ritually clean" (prayer/purity leakage — distinct emotional register; this is the segment most actively discussing the problem within trusted community circles, e.g. mosque women's groups, even while avoiding public search — high-trust community distribution candidate)
  - **B1-D** — "I don't know if this is normal / permanent" (uncertainty + shame)
- **Target person:** Mother 6 wks–12 mo postpartum experiencing bladder leaks, embarrassed, may not have raised it with anyone.
- **Situation:** When a postpartum mother leaks urine, she struggles to stay active and confident because shame + uncertainty keep her silent, and wants a discreet, doable pelvic-floor recovery path.
- **Problem:** Leaks, avoidance of movement, shame, silence.
- **Constraints:** Embarrassment (warm-channel, non-judgemental delivery); clearance for pelvic floor exercise; privacy.
- **Desired transformation:** Regain bladder confidence + a discreet daily pelvic-floor habit.

## 02 — BEFORE STATE
- Experiencing: leaks on laugh/sneeze/run; urgency.
- Doing now: crossing legs, avoiding exercise, pad-dependent.
- Not working: ignoring it (worsens confidence).
- Emotional: shame, isolation ("no one talks about this").
- Practical: limits social/activity.
- Failed attempts: Kegels done wrong / abandoned.
- Environmental: no private space to practise; time-poor.
- People: rarely tells partner/provider.

## 03 — AFTER STATE
- Can now: manage leaks; practise discreet pelvic-floor routine.
- Easier: laugh/run without panic.
- Changed: confidence + reduced shame.
- Reduced: leak frequency / fear.
- System now: daily pelvic-floor micro-habit + leak-management plan.
- Evidence: routine done ≥5×/wk; self-rated confidence up; leak episodes reduced (self-log).

## 04 — TRANSFORMATION MECHANISM
- M1 Normalise: this is common + usually improvable (no shame).
- M2 Teach: correct pelvic-floor engagement (not just "do Kegels").
- M3 Habit: discreet trigger-based micro-practice (e.g. at red lights).
- M4 Manage: leak-plan (pads/route/what-if) + when to see physio.

## 05 — TRANSFORMATION PATH
Normalise → Learn correct engagement → Build habit (triggers) → Manage leaks day-to-day → Review (2 wks) → Escalate to physio if needed → Maintain.
